## Why teams choose us over CompetitorOne

CompetitorOne is a well-known name in the space, and we respect what they have built. But when it comes to shipping a SaaS product quickly without sacrificing quality, our platform gives you a meaningful head start. We bundle the essentials so you can focus on what makes your product unique instead of stitching together third-party services.

Authentication, team management, and billing are not optional extras — they are table stakes. With CompetitorOne you will need to wire up auth yourself or pay for a separate provider, and team-based access control is left as an exercise for the reader. Our starter kit handles all of that out of the box so you can go live in days, not weeks.

Onboarding is where most SaaS products lose their first wave of users. We include automated onboarding flows that guide new customers from signup to their first success moment. CompetitorOne expects you to build this from scratch, which means it often gets deprioritized and your activation rate suffers.

Finally, every plan includes priority support from engineers who have shipped real products. You are not filing tickets into a void — you are talking to people who understand the stack because they built it.
