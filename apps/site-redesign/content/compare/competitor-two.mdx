## Why teams choose us over CompetitorTwo

CompetitorTwo offers a solid foundation for building web applications, but it takes a generalist approach that leaves many SaaS-specific concerns up to you. If you are building a SaaS product, you need more than a generic framework — you need infrastructure designed for subscriptions, tenants, and growth.

Our built-in analytics dashboard means you can track MRR, churn, and user engagement from day one without integrating a third-party service. CompetitorTwo ships without any analytics, so most teams end up bolting on an external tool weeks after launch when they realize they are flying blind.

Email is the backbone of SaaS retention. We include transactional email templates for every critical moment — welcome emails, invoice receipts, password resets, and trial expiration warnings. With CompetitorTwo you will need to set up an email provider, design templates, and manage deliverability yourself.

Webhook management is another area where we pull ahead. Modern SaaS products rely on webhooks for integrations with Stripe, Slack, and dozens of other services. We give you a managed webhook system with retry logic, logging, and signature verification built in. CompetitorTwo leaves this entirely to you, which often means fragile custom code that breaks silently.
