## See Everything That Matters

Most analytics tools bury you in data without telling you what any of it means. Our platform takes a different approach. Every dashboard is designed to surface the metrics that actually drive decisions, not just the ones that are easy to measure.

Set up custom views for each team so engineering sees deployment frequency, marketing sees conversion funnels, and leadership sees the numbers that matter for the next board meeting. No more asking someone to pull a report or waiting for end-of-quarter reviews to find out what happened.

## Stay Ahead With Anomaly Detection

Waiting for something to break before you notice a problem is expensive. Our anomaly detection monitors your key metrics around the clock and flags unusual patterns the moment they appear. Whether it's a sudden spike in churn, a drop in activation rates, or an unexpected surge in API errors, you'll know about it before your customers do.

Alerts are configurable per metric and per team, so you never deal with notification fatigue. Only the right people get notified about the things they can actually act on.

## Reports That Write Themselves

Nobody enjoys building reports from scratch every week. Set up automated reports once and they'll land in the right inboxes on schedule, formatted and ready to share. Pull data from any connected source, combine metrics across tools, and let the platform do the busywork so your team can focus on the analysis.

## Built for Teams That Move Fast

Whether you're a five-person startup or a scaling company with dozens of teams, the analytics layer grows with you. Role-based permissions ensure everyone sees what they need and nothing they don't. Shared dashboards make it easy to align around the same numbers without duplicating work.
