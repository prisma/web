## One Place for Everything

The biggest productivity killer isn't the work itself, it's the time spent figuring out where the work lives. Decisions get buried in Slack threads, feedback gets lost in email, and nobody can find the latest version of anything.

Our collaboration layer gives every project a single home. Tasks, discussions, files, and decisions all live together so your team never has to play detective to find context. When someone new joins a project, they can catch up by reading the thread instead of asking five people what happened.

## Work Together in Real Time

See who's online, who's editing what, and where your teammates are focused, all in real time. Multiplayer editing means two people can work on the same document or task without stepping on each other's toes. Cursor presence and live updates eliminate the "I'll wait until you're done" bottleneck that slows async teams down.

Comments are threaded and attached directly to the thing they're about. No more screenshots in Slack with arrows pointing to the problem. Click a comment, see the context, and respond in place.

## Permissions That Make Sense

Not everyone needs access to everything. Set permissions at the workspace, project, or document level so each team member sees exactly what's relevant to their role. External collaborators like clients or contractors can be given scoped access without seeing your internal discussions.

## Stay in the Loop Without the Noise

Activity feeds give you a chronological view of everything happening across your projects. Filter by team, project, or person to zero in on what matters. Notifications are smart by default, surfacing mentions and assignments while keeping routine updates out of your way.
