## Your Tools, Connected

Switching between tabs and copy-pasting data between apps is a hidden tax on your team's productivity. Our integration layer connects the platforms you already rely on so information flows automatically. When a deal closes in your CRM, your project management tool knows. When a payment fails in Stripe, the right people get notified instantly.

Setup takes minutes, not sprints. Every integration uses OAuth so there's no need to manage API keys or bother your engineering team. Connect an app, configure what data you want to sync, and you're done.

## Automate the Handoffs

The most painful bottlenecks in any workflow happen at the boundaries between tools. A designer finishes a mockup in Figma, but the ticket in your project tracker doesn't update. A customer submits a support request, but the context from their account doesn't follow.

Our integrations handle these handoffs automatically. Define triggers and actions across any connected tool, and let the platform move data where it needs to go without anyone lifting a finger.

## Build Custom Connections

For workflows that are unique to your team, webhooks and our REST API give you full control. Push events to any endpoint, pull data into your own dashboards, or use Zapier to connect with thousands of additional apps. If the integration you need doesn't exist yet, you can build it in an afternoon.

## Data Stays in Sync

Stale data is worse than no data. Every integration is built on real-time or near-real-time sync so your team is always working with the latest information. Conflict resolution is handled automatically, and a detailed sync log lets you audit exactly what changed and when.
