## Stop waiting on engineering tickets

Every marketer knows the pain of filing a Jira ticket for a one-line copy change and waiting three sprints for it to ship. Our visual editor and content management tools put you in control. Update headlines, swap images, publish new landing pages, and run experiments — all without writing a single line of code.

## One dashboard to rule them all

Forget switching between your analytics tool, your email platform, your ad manager, and your CMS. Our unified dashboard brings all your marketing data into a single view. See which channels drive conversions, track campaign ROI in real time, and build custom reports that answer the questions your team actually asks.

## Funnels that don't leak

From first touch to closed deal, every step of your funnel lives on one platform. Lead capture forms, automated nurture sequences, scoring, and handoff to sales — it all connects natively. No more lost leads because a webhook failed or a Zapier step timed out.

## Test everything, guess nothing

Built-in A/B testing lets you experiment with headlines, CTAs, layouts, and offers without involving your dev team. Statistical significance is calculated automatically, and winning variants can be promoted with a single click. Your intuition is great, but data is better.
