## Move fast without breaking things

Startups live and die by execution speed. Every hour your team spends wrestling with infrastructure is an hour not spent talking to customers or shipping features. Our platform gives you the foundation so you can focus on what actually matters — building something people want.

## From prototype to production in a weekend

We've taken the patterns that the best engineering teams use and packaged them into a starting point that works out of the box. Authentication, billing, team management, analytics — it's all there. Fork it, customize it, and deploy. Your first version can be live before Monday morning.

The architecture underneath is the same one powering companies at 100x your current traffic. You won't need to rewrite anything when things take off. Database connections pool automatically, background jobs scale horizontally, and CDN caching keeps your pages fast no matter where your users are.
