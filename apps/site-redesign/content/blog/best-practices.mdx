After working with hundreds of teams, we've identified patterns that separate good teams from great ones. Here are our top recommendations.

## 1. Start with a Clear Structure

Organize your projects by team or function. A consistent naming convention goes a long way.

## 2. Use Templates

Don't reinvent the wheel. Create templates for recurring workflows and share them across your team.

## 3. Set Up Notifications Wisely

Turn on notifications for what matters. Turn off everything else. Signal over noise.

## 4. Automate Repetitive Tasks

Use our API and integrations to automate the work nobody wants to do manually.

## 5. Review Weekly

Set aside 15 minutes each week to review your dashboard. Small course corrections prevent big problems.

## 6. Document Decisions

Use comments and notes to capture why decisions were made, not just what was decided.

## 7. Keep Projects Focused

One project, one goal. Avoid scope creep by creating separate projects for separate initiatives.

## 8. Invite the Right People

Not everyone needs access to everything. Use roles and permissions to keep things clean.

## 9. Archive When Done

Don't let finished projects clutter your workspace. Archive them to keep your dashboard focused.

## 10. Share Feedback

We build based on what you tell us. Use the feedback button to let us know what's working and what isn't.
