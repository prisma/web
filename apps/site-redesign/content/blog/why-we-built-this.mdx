Every product starts with a frustration. Ours was no different.

## The Problem

We spent years working with tools that were either too complex for small teams or too simple for real work. There was no middle ground — no tool that was powerful enough to handle serious workflows but simple enough to pick up in an afternoon.

## Our Approach

We set out to build something different:

- **Simple by default** — No configuration required to get started
- **Powerful when needed** — Advanced features are there when you need them
- **Fast everywhere** — Performance is a feature, not an afterthought

## What We Learned

Building in public taught us that the best products come from tight feedback loops. We shipped early, listened carefully, and iterated quickly.

> "The best way to predict the future is to build it." — Alan Kay

## What's Next

We're just getting started. Our roadmap is driven by what our users need, and we're committed to shipping improvements every week.
