Acme Corp had built a thriving e-commerce business, but their conversion rates had plateaued. Despite driving significant traffic through paid ads and organic search, only a small fraction of visitors were completing purchases. Their team knew something had to change, but lacked the tools to identify exactly where customers were dropping off.

## The Challenge

The biggest pain point was visibility. Acme Corp's existing analytics setup gave them high-level numbers, but nothing actionable. They couldn't tell whether the problem was page load speed, confusing checkout flows, or poor product recommendations. Their engineering team was stretched thin maintaining a patchwork of internal tools, leaving little bandwidth for optimization work.

## The Solution

After evaluating several platforms, Acme Corp chose our solution for its out-of-the-box analytics, A/B testing capabilities, and seamless integration with their existing Shopify storefront. Within a week, their team had full visibility into their conversion funnel and was running their first experiments.

## The Results

The impact was immediate. In the first month, Acme Corp identified three critical drop-off points in their checkout flow. By the end of the second month, they had tested and deployed fixes for each one. By month three, their conversion rate had climbed by 150% compared to their baseline — translating to an additional $2.3 million in quarterly revenue.

## Looking Ahead

Today, Acme Corp runs continuous optimization experiments across their entire storefront. Their team credits the platform with freeing up engineering resources and giving their marketing team the autonomy to test ideas without developer support. As their CTO put it, "We went from guessing to knowing — and the numbers speak for themselves."
