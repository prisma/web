Globex had grown from a scrappy startup into a mid-market SaaS company with over 200 employees. But their deployment process hadn't scaled with them. What once took a few minutes now required hours of manual coordination, and failed deployments were becoming a weekly occurrence. Feature releases were slowing down, and customer frustration was mounting.

## The Challenge

Globex's engineering team relied on a fragile CI/CD pipeline stitched together from open-source tools and custom scripts. Every deployment required a dedicated engineer to babysit the process, and rollbacks were painful. The team was spending more time fighting their infrastructure than building product, and morale was starting to suffer.

## The Solution

Globex adopted our platform to replace their brittle deployment pipeline. The migration was straightforward — their team connected their existing GitHub repositories and configured deployment rules in an afternoon. Automated rollbacks, staged rollouts, and real-time monitoring were available from day one, with zero changes to their application code.

## The Results

Within the first sprint, Globex went from deploying once a week to multiple times per day. Their average deployment time dropped from 45 minutes to under 15, and failed deployments fell by 90%. The engineering team reclaimed over 20 hours per week that had previously been lost to deployment overhead, redirecting that time toward feature development and technical debt reduction.

## Looking Ahead

Globex now ships features three times faster than before the switch. Their VP of Engineering estimates the platform has saved the company the equivalent of two full-time engineering hires. More importantly, their team has regained confidence in their release process, and customer satisfaction scores have risen alongside their shipping velocity.
