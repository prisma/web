Initech had built a powerful financial analytics platform, but they had a retention problem. Monthly churn was hovering around 8%, which meant they were effectively replacing a third of their customer base every year. Growth was stalling because acquisition couldn't keep pace with the customers walking out the door.

## The Challenge

The root cause wasn't obvious. Initech's product had strong reviews, and their NPS score was respectable. But when they dug deeper, they found that most churned customers had never fully adopted the platform's core features. Onboarding was technically complete after account setup, but users weren't discovering the workflows that would make the product indispensable. Without clear engagement data, Initech's customer success team was flying blind.

## The Solution

Initech integrated our platform to gain granular visibility into user behavior and engagement patterns. They set up automated health scores for every account, triggered proactive outreach when engagement dipped, and built personalized onboarding sequences tailored to each customer's use case. The entire setup took less than two weeks, and their customer success team was able to manage it without engineering support.

## The Results

The turnaround was dramatic. Within three months, Initech's monthly churn rate dropped from 8% to under 5%. By the six-month mark, it had fallen to 3.2% — a 60% reduction. More importantly, customers who went through the new onboarding flow showed 40% higher feature adoption rates, which directly correlated with longer retention and higher lifetime value.

## Looking Ahead

Initech has since expanded their use of the platform to power upsell and expansion revenue workflows. Their customer success team now operates as a revenue center rather than a cost center, and annual retention rates have climbed above 90% for the first time in company history. As their CEO noted, "Fixing churn didn't just save our existing revenue — it unlocked a growth flywheel we didn't know we had."
