Prisma 8 is a [release candidate](/orm/release-status). Each guide below keeps your database and its data, and ends with the app running only on Prisma 8. Pick the one that matches your database, the version you run today, and how you want to move:

| Database | You run | Strategy | Guide |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| PostgreSQL | Prisma ORM 6 or 7 | All at once: keep your `schema.prisma` and rewrite every query in one pass | [Prisma ORM 6 or 7 to 8 (PostgreSQL)](/guides/upgrade-prisma-orm/postgresql) |
| PostgreSQL | Prisma ORM 7 | Route by route: run Prisma ORM 7 and Prisma 8 side by side and move one route at a time | [Prisma ORM 7 to 8, route by route (PostgreSQL)](/guides/upgrade-prisma-orm/postgresql-incremental) |
| MongoDB | Prisma ORM 6 | All at once (Prisma ORM 7 has no MongoDB connector) | [Prisma ORM 6 to 8 (MongoDB)](/guides/upgrade-prisma-orm/mongodb) |
| Any | Prisma ORM 6 or earlier | Staying on Prisma ORM 7 for now | [Upgrade to Prisma ORM 7](/guides/upgrade-prisma-orm/v7) |

The two PostgreSQL guides also differ in where the [contract](/orm/contract-authoring/the-data-contract) comes from. The all-at-once guide reads your existing `schema.prisma` through `prisma7Schema(...)`, so you keep editing that file. The route-by-route guide infers a new `contract.prisma` from the live database.

## Deploying on Prisma Compute

Prisma Compute runs only Prisma 8, so finish the upgrade before you deploy there. An app in the middle of the route-by-route guide still runs Prisma ORM 7 and cannot deploy on Compute until that guide's last step removes it. Once the upgrade is done, follow [Deploy an existing app on Prisma Compute](/guides/compute/deploy-existing-app).
