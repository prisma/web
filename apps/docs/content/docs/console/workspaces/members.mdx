A workspace can have several members, and every member can see and manage everything in it: projects, databases, billing, and other members. You manage members under **Workspace Settings > Members**.

## Roles and permissions

Every member of a workspace is an Admin. There is no role picker when you invite someone, and there are no read-only or billing-only roles.

Workspaces also have no owner. The person who created a workspace has the same permissions as everyone else, so any member can remove any other member, including the creator.

## Invite a member

Any member can invite someone:

1. Open the workspace in the [Console](https://console.prisma.io).
2. Go to **Workspace Settings > Members**.
3. Click **Invite member**, enter the person's email address, and send the invite.

The person receives an invite email. If they don't have a Prisma account yet, they need to create one with that same email before they accept. See [Sign in to the Console](/console/account/sign-in).

## Accept an invite

Open the invite email and accept it while signed in to the Console. Two rules apply:

- The email of the account you're signed in with must match the invited email exactly. If you signed in with GitHub, that's your GitHub account's primary email.
- The invite expires after 7 days. Ask a member to send a new one if yours expired.

After you accept, the workspace appears in your workspace switcher.

If an invite seems not to arrive, check whether it was sent to the address you sign in with. An invite to a work address can't be accepted from an account that uses a personal address.

## Remove a member or leave a workspace

- To remove someone, go to **Workspace Settings > Members** and click **Remove member** next to them.
- To remove yourself, choose **Leave workspace**.

The last member of a workspace can't be removed and can't leave. To get rid of a workspace you're the only member of, [delete the workspace](/console/account/delete-account#delete-a-workspace) instead.

Removing a member doesn't change the workspace's billing email. If the billing email belongs to the person leaving, update it under **Workspace Settings > Billing > Manage billing information**. See [Invoices and payments](/console/billing/invoices-and-payments).

## Transfer ownership

Because workspaces have no owner, there's nothing to transfer. To hand a workspace to someone else:

1. Invite them as a member.
2. Once they've accepted, remove yourself with **Leave workspace**, or let them remove you.
3. Update the billing email if it's yours.

The same steps move your own workspaces to a new email address. See [Change your account email](/console/account/change-email).

## Regain access when no member is reachable

If someone needs access to a company workspace, the fastest route is to ask any current member to invite them.

When no member can be reached, for example because everyone who had access has left the company, contact [support@prisma.io](mailto:support@prisma.io).

If your old credential is a key for the retired Prisma Data Proxy (connection strings on `prisma-data.com`), it doesn't correspond to a Console workspace, so there's no workspace to recover.

## Move a project to another workspace

You can move a project to another workspace with the Prisma CLI or the REST API, but not from the Console. All of the project's databases move with it, and their connection strings keep working.

- **CLI:** run `npx prisma project transfer <project>` with `--to-workspace <workspace>`. You need access to both workspaces. See [project](/cli/project).
- **REST API:** call [`POST /v1/projects/{id}/transfer`](/rest-api/endpoints/projects/post-projects-by-id-transfer) with a token for the current workspace, and pass a token for the target workspace as `recipientAccessToken`.

A project that contains Prisma Compute apps can't be transferred yet. The transfer also fails if the target workspace's plan doesn't allow that many databases.

Databases can't be moved between projects. To move a database's data to a different project, create a new database in the target project, export the data from the old one, and import it into the new one. See [Backups](/postgres/database/backups) for exporting with `pg_dump`.

## Vercel Marketplace workspaces

A workspace created from the Vercel Marketplace is named after the Vercel team. **Open in Prisma** in the Vercel dashboard signs you in to the Console, and signing in directly with the same email shows the same workspace. See [Vercel Marketplace](/console/billing/vercel-marketplace).

## Common questions

### How do I make someone an admin or owner of our workspace?

Invite them. Every member is an Admin, and there's no owner role, so a new member has the same permissions as everyone else.

### My teammate's invite never arrived. What should I check?

Confirm the invite went to the email they sign in with, and that it's less than 7 days old. The account accepting must use the exact invited email; send a new invite if it expired.

### The person who created our workspace left the company. How do we get it back?

If anyone else is still a member, they can invite you. If no one is, contact support; see [Regain access when no member is reachable](#regain-access-when-no-member-is-reachable).

### Can another member remove me from a workspace I created?

Yes. Workspaces have no owner, and any member can remove any other member, including the creator. Only the last member can't be removed.

### How do I transfer ownership of my workspace?

There's no owner to transfer. Invite the new person, then leave the workspace or let them remove you, and update the billing email.

### Can I move a project or database to a different workspace?

You can move a whole project, with its databases, using `prisma project transfer` or the REST API, but not from the Console, and not if the project has Prisma Compute apps. See [Move a project to another workspace](#move-a-project-to-another-workspace). A single database can't be moved between projects; export and import the data instead.
