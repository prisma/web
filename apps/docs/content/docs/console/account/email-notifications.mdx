Prisma sends several kinds of email: tips while you set up, check-ins when a workspace goes quiet, alerts when you approach a usage or spend limit, and billing emails. Each kind is turned off in a different place. Use the table to find the email you received, then follow the section for it.

| Kind of email                | Example subject                                                        | How to stop it                                                                                   |
| ---------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| Onboarding                   | "Continue setting up your Prisma project"                              | Unsubscribe link in the email, or **Workspace Settings > Notifications**                         |
| Re-engagement (inactivity)   | "Still using Prisma?", "Continue where you left off in Prisma"         | **Workspace Settings > Notifications**, clear **Reactivation emails** (not the unsubscribe link) |
| Usage and spend limit alerts | "Action Required: Update Limits Now to Maintain Uninterrupted Service" | Can't be turned off; see [Usage and spend limit alerts](#usage-and-spend-limit-alerts)           |
| Billing                      | Invoices, payment failures                                             | Can't be turned off; see [Billing emails](#billing-emails)                                       |
| Product news and marketing   | Product updates, announcements                                         | Unsubscribe link in the email                                                                    |

Emails about your account go to your account email. Limit alerts and payment-failure emails go to every member of the workspace. Invoices go to the workspace's billing email, which can be a different address.

## Onboarding emails

After you create a workspace, Prisma sends a short series of emails to help you finish setting up:

| Email                                     | Sent when                                                         |
| ----------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------- |
| "Set up Prisma with your agent"           | 3 days after the workspace was created, if setup hasn't started   |
| "Still want to try Prisma?"               | 14 days after the workspace was created, if setup hasn't started  |
| "Continue setting up your Prisma project" | 1 day after setup started, if there's no usage or deployment yet  |
| "Want help finishing your Prisma setup?"  | 3 days after setup started, if there's no usage or deployment yet |
| "What got in the way?"                    | 7 days after setup started. It's a short survey.                  |

To stop them, click the unsubscribe link in any onboarding email. It opens a page titled "Stop onboarding emails?". Confirm there, and onboarding emails stop for that workspace.

You can reply to these emails. Replies reach Prisma support, so if something got in the way, describe the problem and you'll get an answer.

## Re-engagement emails, including "Still using Prisma?"

Two emails check in when a workspace goes quiet:

| Email                                   | Sent when                                                          |
| --------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| "Continue where you left off in Prisma" | The workspace had usage in the last 30 days but none in the last 7 |
| "Still using Prisma?"                   | The workspace has had no database operations for 30 days           |

These are check-ins, so "Still using Prisma?" doesn't mean your database is about to be paused or deleted: the database stays available, and any new activity resets the 30-day window.

The unsubscribe link in onboarding emails doesn't stop these two. Turn them off per workspace:

1. Open the workspace in the [Console](https://console.prisma.io).
2. Go to **Workspace Settings > Notifications**.
3. Clear **Reactivation emails**.

Repeat for each workspace that sends them. You can also reply to "Still using Prisma?" with "blocked", "paused", or "moved", or a sentence about what's going on. You don't need to reply to keep your database.

## Usage and spend limit alerts

Prisma emails you when a workspace hits or approaches a limit:

| Email                                                                  | Sent when                                                       |
| ---------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------- |
| "Action Required: Upgrade Now to Maintain Uninterrupted Service"       | A Free workspace reaches its monthly plan limit and is blocked  |
| "Action Required: Update Limits Now to Maintain Uninterrupted Service" | A paid workspace reaches 75% of its spend limit                 |
| "Action Required: Update Limits Now to Restore Service"                | A paid workspace reaches 100% of its spend limit and is blocked |

A workspace without a spend limit gets neither spend limit email. See [Spend limits](/console/billing/spend-limits) and [Usage limits and blocks](/console/billing/usage-limits-and-blocks).

Every member of the workspace receives these alerts, not only the person who created it or the billing email. You can't turn them off. They aren't listed under **Workspace Settings > Notifications**, because they warn that the workspace is about to stop working or already has. To stop receiving them for a workspace, leave the workspace.

## Billing emails

Billing emails can't be turned off.

- Invoices and receipts go to the workspace's billing email only. To send them somewhere else, change the billing email under **Workspace Settings > Billing > Manage billing information**. See [Invoices and payments](/console/billing/invoices-and-payments).
- Payment-failure emails ("Action Required: Payment Required to…") and the "Your payment has been received" follow-up go to every member of the workspace, and also to the billing email when it isn't a member's address.

For a Vercel Marketplace workspace, Vercel sends the invoices and the billing email follows the Vercel team's contact. See [Vercel Marketplace](/console/billing/vercel-marketplace).

## Product news and marketing

When you sign up, you agree to receive occasional product update emails from Prisma. To stop them, use the unsubscribe link at the bottom of any of these emails. There's no Console setting for them.

Product updates and onboarding emails are unsubscribed separately. Unsubscribing from product updates doesn't stop onboarding emails, and the unsubscribe link in an onboarding email doesn't stop product updates.

## Common questions

### I clicked unsubscribe but I still get "Still using Prisma?" emails. Why?

The unsubscribe link only stops onboarding emails. Turn off "Still using Prisma?" under **Workspace Settings > Notifications** by clearing **Reactivation emails**, in each workspace.

### Is Prisma going to pause or delete my database because I haven't used it?

The "Still using Prisma?" email is a check-in, not a warning. Your database stays available, and any new activity resets the 30-day window.

### How do I stop all emails from Prisma?

Use the unsubscribe link for onboarding emails and for product updates, and clear **Reactivation emails** under **Workspace Settings > Notifications** for re-engagement emails. Limit alerts and billing emails can't be turned off while you're a member of the workspace (see [Billing emails](#billing-emails)). To stop everything, [delete your account](/console/account/delete-account).

### Can I get these emails at a different address?

They go to your account email, and the Console can't change it. Move to an account with the new address using [Change your account email](/console/account/change-email). For invoices, update the billing email instead.

### I replied "unsubscribe" to an email. Is that enough?

A reply reaches a person, not an automatic unsubscribe. Use the link or the Notifications setting described above so the change takes effect.

### I meant to log out, not unsubscribe. How do I sign out of the Console?

Click your name at the bottom of the Console sidebar to open the account menu, then click **Sign out**. Unsubscribing from emails doesn't sign you out, and signing out doesn't change which emails you get.
