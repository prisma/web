The Console has no setting to change the email on an account, and support can't change it for you either. The email on **Account Settings > Profile** is read-only. To move to a new address, you create a second account with the new email and give it access to your workspaces from the old account.

You do every step yourself while signed in, so no identity check is needed. Plan for a few minutes per workspace.

## Prerequisites

- You can still sign in to the old account.
- You can receive email at the new address.

If you've lost access to the old account (for example, a school or work address that was deactivated), you can't use this route. See [Regain access to a workspace](/console/workspaces/members#regain-access-when-no-member-is-reachable).

## 1. Create an account with the new email

Sign up at [console.prisma.io](https://console.prisma.io) with the new email address. Use email and password, or a GitHub or Google account whose email is the new address. See [Sign in to the Console](/console/account/sign-in) for how accounts are matched by email.

## 2. Invite the new account from the old one

Sign in with the old account, open the workspace, and go to **Workspace Settings > Members**. Click **Invite member**, enter the new email, and send the invite.

Every member of a workspace has full access, so there is no role to pick. See [Workspace members](/console/workspaces/members) for how roles work.

## 3. Accept the invite

Sign in with the new account and accept the invite from the email. The email of the account accepting the invite must match the invited address exactly, and the invite expires after 7 days. If it expires, send a new one from the old account.

You should now see the workspace in the new account's workspace switcher.

## 4. Remove the old account

Once the new account has access, remove the old one from the workspace. Either:

- From the new account, go to **Workspace Settings > Members** and click **Remove member** next to the old account, or
- From the old account, choose **Leave workspace**.

Repeat steps 2 to 4 for every workspace the old account belongs to.

## 5. Update the billing email

Removing the old user doesn't change the workspace's billing email. Until you change it, invoices and payment-failure emails keep going to the old address.

Go to **Workspace Settings > Billing**, click **Manage billing information**, and enter the new email. Do this in each workspace you pay for. See [Invoices and payments](/console/billing/invoices-and-payments) for more on the billing email.

For a workspace created through the Vercel Marketplace, the billing email follows the Vercel team's contact email and can't be edited in the Console. Change it in Vercel. See [Vercel Marketplace](/console/billing/vercel-marketplace).

## 6. Delete the old account (optional)

When the old account is no longer a member of any workspace you need, you can delete it. See [Delete your account](/console/account/delete-account).

## Common questions

### Can support change my email for me?

No. There is no email-change tool for staff either, so the invite-and-remove steps above are the only way to move to a new address.

### My old email is being deactivated. What should I do first?

Do the steps on this page while you can still sign in with the old address. Once it's gone, you can't send invites from it.

### Will I lose my databases or projects?

No. The databases and projects belong to the workspace, not to your user account. Moving the workspace to a new member account doesn't touch them.

### I changed accounts but invoices still go to my old email. Why?

The billing email is set separately from your account email. Update it under **Workspace Settings > Billing > Manage billing information**.

### Can I get Prisma emails at a different address without moving my account?

Product and lifecycle emails go to your account email. To receive them elsewhere, move to a new account with the steps above. See [Email notifications](/console/account/email-notifications).

### The invite never arrived or says it's invalid. What now?

Check that you're signed in with the account whose email matches the invited address, and that the invite is less than 7 days old. If it expired, send a new one.
