You can delete your user account, a workspace, a project, or a database from the Console without contacting support. This page gives the Console path for each, what the deletion removes, and the conditions that block it.

:::danger
Export any data you want to keep before you delete a database, project, workspace, or account. See [Before you delete: export your data](#before-you-delete-export-your-data).
:::

## Summary

| What to delete | Where in the Console                                   | Blocked when                                                                                                                                                         |
| -------------- | ------------------------------------------------------ | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| User account   | **Account Settings > Profile > Delete Account**        | A workspace where you're the only member is on a paid plan, has an unpaid invoice, has active Prisma Compute deployments, or is managed by a marketplace such as Vercel. |
| Workspace      | **Workspace Settings > Overview > Delete workspace**   | It's your last workspace, it's on a paid plan, it has an unpaid invoice, it has active deployments, or a marketplace manages it.                                      |
| Project        | Project **Settings > Delete Project**                  | The workspace is managed by a marketplace, or the project has Netlify or Stripe Projects resources.                                                                  |
| Database       | Database **Settings > Delete Database**                | It's the project's default database, or the workspace is managed by Vercel.                                                                                          |

## Delete your user account

Go to **Account Settings > Profile** and click **Delete Account**. This works the same way whichever method you signed up with (GitHub, Google, or email and password).

### What gets deleted

- Your user account.
- Every workspace where you're the only member, together with what's in it.

### What isn't deleted

- Workspaces that have other members. You're removed from them, and the other members keep the workspace, its projects, and its databases.
- The billing email set on a shared workspace. If it's your address, ask a remaining member to change it under **Workspace Settings > Billing > Manage billing information**.

### What blocks account deletion

Account deletion is refused while any workspace where you're the only member:

- is on a paid plan. [Cancel the plan](/console/billing/change-or-cancel-plan) first.
- has an unpaid invoice. Pay it first; see [Invoices and payments](/console/billing/invoices-and-payments).
- has active [Prisma Compute](/compute) deployments. Remove them first.
- is managed by a marketplace such as Vercel. Manage it from the marketplace instead; see [Vercel Marketplace](/console/billing/vercel-marketplace).

Support deletes accounts under the same rules, so clear the blocker before you ask for help.

## Delete a workspace

Go to **Workspace Settings > Overview** and click **Delete workspace**.

Deletion is refused when:

- It's your last workspace. Every account keeps at least one; delete your account instead if you want to remove everything.
- It's on a paid plan. Cancel the plan first. A cancellation takes effect at the end of the current billing period, so the workspace counts as paid until that period ends.
- It has an unpaid invoice.
- It has active deployments.
- A marketplace, such as Vercel, manages it.

## Delete a project

Open the project, go to **Settings**, and click **Delete Project**.

Deletion is refused when:

- The workspace is managed by a marketplace such as Vercel.
- The project has resources managed by Netlify or Stripe Projects. Remove them from that service first.

## Delete a database

Open the database, go to **Settings**, and click **Delete Database**.

Deletion is refused when:

- It's the project's default database. Delete the project instead if you want to remove it.
- The workspace is managed by Vercel. See [Vercel Marketplace](/console/billing/vercel-marketplace).

## What deletion removes

Deleting a project deletes every database in it, together with their connection strings, and removes any Prisma Compute apps in the project. Deleting a workspace deletes all of its projects and their databases. The Console removes them straight away. The databases themselves are then deleted in the background, so this can finish shortly after the workspace disappears from the Console.

Invoices and payment records are kept by our payment processor after deletion, so past invoices remain available for accounting. For a formal data-erasure request under GDPR, email [dpo@prisma.io](mailto:dpo@prisma.io).

## Can a deletion be undone?

Deletion is permanent. There is no way to restore a deleted account, workspace, project, or database from the Console, and deleting a database also removes access to its backups. Export anything you want to keep before you delete it (see [Before you delete: export your data](#before-you-delete-export-your-data)).

## Before you delete: export your data

Deleting a database deletes its data. To keep a copy, create a backup file with `pg_dump` over a direct connection before you delete anything. See [Backups](/postgres/database/backups) for the steps.

If your Free workspace is blocked because it reached its plan limit, the database doesn't accept queries, so an export fails. See [Usage limits and blocks](/console/billing/usage-limits-and-blocks) for how to regain access long enough to export.

## Data erasure requests

There is no separate data-erasure process in the Console. Deleting your account removes your user and the workspaces where you're the only member. For a formal GDPR erasure or other data subject request, email [dpo@prisma.io](mailto:dpo@prisma.io). See also [Security and compliance](/console/more/security-and-compliance#gdpr-and-data-subject-requests).

## Common questions

### How do I delete my Prisma account?

Go to **Account Settings > Profile** and click **Delete Account**. You don't need to email support. If deletion is refused, one of your sole-member workspaces is paid, has an unpaid invoice, has active Compute deployments, or is managed by a marketplace.

### Do I still need to disable Accelerate or email support to delete my account?

No. Older instructions asked you to disable Accelerate and email support. Account deletion is now self-service under **Account Settings > Profile > Delete Account**, with the blockers listed above.

### Why can't I delete my workspace?

It's either your last workspace, on a paid plan (including a cancelled plan that hasn't reached the end of its billing period), has an unpaid invoice, has active deployments, or is managed by a marketplace. Clear the blocker and try again.

### If I delete my account, does my team lose our shared workspace?

No. Workspaces with other members are kept. Only the workspaces where you're the only member are deleted.

### Why can't I delete this project?

The workspace is managed by a marketplace such as Vercel, or the project has resources managed by Netlify or Stripe Projects. Remove those resources from that service first.

### Why can't I delete this database?

It's the project's default database, or the workspace is managed by Vercel. Delete the whole project to remove a default database. For Vercel-managed workspaces, see [Vercel Marketplace](/console/billing/vercel-marketplace).

### Can I get my database back after deleting it?

Not from the Console. Deletion is permanent, and deleting a database also removes access to its backups. Export your data with `pg_dump` before you delete it.
