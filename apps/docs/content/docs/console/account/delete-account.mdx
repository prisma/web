You can delete your user account, a workspace, a project, or a database from the Console without contacting support. This page gives the Console path for each, what the deletion removes, and the conditions that block it.

:::danger
Export any data you want to keep before you delete a database, project, workspace, or account. See [Before you delete: export your data](#before-you-delete-export-your-data).
:::

To open a workspace's settings, switch to that workspace with the workspace switcher at the top left of the Console, then go to **Workspace Settings**.

## Summary

| What to delete | Where in the Console                                                   | Blocked when                                                                                                                                                                                                                  |
| -------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| User account   | Your name at the bottom of the sidebar > **Account settings** > **Delete Account** | A workspace where you're the only member is on a paid plan, has an unpaid invoice, has Accelerate enabled, has Prisma Compute deployments, or is managed through an integration such as Vercel. See [the full list](#if-account-deletion-is-blocked). |
| Workspace      | **Workspace Settings > Overview > Delete workspace**                   | It's your last workspace, it's on a paid plan, it has an unpaid invoice, it has active Prisma Compute deployments, or an integration manages it.                                                                              |
| Project        | Project **Settings > Delete Project**                                  | The workspace is managed through the Vercel Marketplace, or the project has Netlify or Stripe Projects resources.                                                                                                             |
| Database       | Database **Settings > Delete Database**                                | It's the project's default database, or it was created through the Vercel Marketplace.                                                                                                                                        |

## Delete your user account

Your Prisma account is the one you sign in to the [Prisma Console](https://console.prisma.io) with. Deletion is permanent and can't be undone. It works the same way whichever method you signed up with (GitHub, Google, or email and password).

1. Open the [Prisma Console](https://console.prisma.io), click your name at the bottom of the sidebar, and choose **Account settings**.
2. In the **Delete Account** section, click **Delete Account**.
3. In the **Delete your account** dialog, type `DELETE` in capitals and click **Delete Account**.

Console deletes your account and signs you out.

### What gets deleted

- Your user account.
- Every workspace where you're the only member, together with its projects and databases.

### What isn't deleted

- Workspaces that have other members. You're removed from them, and the other members keep the workspace, its projects, and its databases.
- The billing email set on a shared workspace. If it's your address, ask a remaining member to change it under **Workspace Settings > Billing > Manage billing information** before you delete your account, or invoices for that workspace keep going to it.

### If account deletion is blocked

Deletion stops if any of the causes below apply, and each message starts with the name of the workspace that causes it. Some workspaces are *managed through an integration*, meaning they were created from the Prisma integration in Vercel or from Stripe Projects. If one of those blocks you, the **Delete Account** button is disabled and shows why. Anything else blocks you after you click **Delete Account**: a dialog lists every reason. Fix each one, then try again.

| Cause | Message in Console |
| --- | --- |
| A workspace where you are the only member is managed through an integration. | "Transfer or remove the integration-managed workspace before deleting your account." |
| A shared workspace managed through an integration uses your email as its billing email. | "Update the workspace billing email before deleting your account." |
| A workspace where you are the only member is on a paid plan. | "Downgrade to the Free plan before deleting your account." |
| A workspace where you are the only member has unpaid invoices. | "Please resolve your outstanding balance before deleting your account." |
| Accelerate is enabled on a database in a workspace where you are the only member. | "Disable Accelerate on all databases before deleting your account." |
| A workspace where you are the only member has Prisma Compute deployments, including stopped ones. | "Stop and delete all compute instances before deleting your account." |
| A workspace's GitHub connection was set up under your account, even if you have since left that workspace. | "Ask another admin of that workspace to reconnect GitHub before deleting your account." |

How to clear each cause:

- **Integration-managed workspace.** Console can't delete a workspace that is managed through an integration. For a workspace created from the Prisma integration in Vercel, uninstall the integration in Vercel. This deletes the Prisma workspace the integration created and all its databases, so export any data you want to keep first. [How do I delete a Prisma Postgres database I created through Vercel?](/postgres/faq#how-do-i-delete-a-prisma-postgres-database-i-created-through-vercel) has the uninstall steps and covers deleting a single database. If you can't transfer or remove it from the integration side, email [support@prisma.io](mailto:support@prisma.io) with the workspace name.
- **Billing email.** You can't change the billing email of an integration-managed workspace in Console. Email [support@prisma.io](mailto:support@prisma.io) with the workspace name.
- **Paid plan.** Go to **Workspace Settings > Billing > Manage subscription**, choose **Free**, and click **Schedule cancellation**. The downgrade takes effect at the end of the current billing period, so you can't delete your account until then. When the **Subscription** card on the Billing page says "Your workspace is on the Free plan", the downgrade is done. See [Cancel a paid plan](/console/billing/change-or-cancel-plan#cancel-a-paid-plan).
- **Unpaid invoices.** Click **Pay your outstanding balance** in the banner at the top of the Console to open the unpaid invoice and pay it, or go to **Workspace Settings > Billing** and click **View Invoices**. See [When a payment fails](/console/billing/invoices-and-payments#when-a-payment-fails).
- **Accelerate.** In each affected workspace, open the database, click **Accelerate** in its sidebar, open the **Settings** tab, and click **Disable Accelerate**.
- **Prisma Compute.** The Console message calls Prisma Compute deployments compute instances. In each project of the affected workspace, under **Apps** in the project sidebar, open the app's **Deployments** page, and from each deployment's actions menu choose **Stop deployment** and then **Destroy deployment**. If a deployment is already stopped, destroy it, because stopped deployments still count. To remove the app and all its deployments at once, choose **Delete app** from the app's menu.
- **GitHub.** Ask another admin of that workspace to disconnect and reconnect GitHub, so the connection is no longer recorded under your account. They open **Workspace Settings > Integrations**, choose **Disconnect this installation** from the installation's menu, and connect GitHub again with **Connect your GitHub account** or **Install Prisma on GitHub**.

If Accelerate or Prisma Compute is the only thing blocking deletion in a workspace where you are the only member, you can [delete that workspace](#delete-a-workspace) instead.

Support deletes accounts under the same rules, so clear the blocker before you ask for help. If you can't resolve a blocker from Console, email [support@prisma.io](mailto:support@prisma.io).

## Delete a workspace

Go to **Workspace Settings > Overview**, click **Delete workspace**, and type the workspace name to confirm. This permanently deletes the workspace with all of its projects and databases.

The button is disabled, and shows why, when any of these apply:

| Cause | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| The workspace is on a paid plan. | [Cancel the paid plan](/console/billing/change-or-cancel-plan#cancel-a-paid-plan) and wait for the cancellation to take effect at the end of the billing period. Until then, the workspace counts as paid. |
| The workspace has an unpaid invoice. | Pay it. See [When a payment fails](/console/billing/invoices-and-payments#when-a-payment-fails). |
| It is the only workspace on your account. | Every account keeps at least one workspace. To remove your last one, [delete your account](#delete-your-user-account). |
| The workspace is managed through an integration, such as the Vercel Marketplace. | Remove it from the integration's side. See [Vercel Marketplace billing](/console/billing/vercel-marketplace#deleting-databases-and-uninstalling). |

Deletion also stops if the workspace has active Prisma Compute deployments. Stop and delete them, then try again.

If you are the last member of a workspace, [leaving it](/console/workspaces/members#remove-a-member-or-leave-a-workspace) also deletes it, under the same conditions.

## Delete a project

1. Open the project and go to its **Settings** tab.
2. Click **Delete Project**, type the project name to confirm, and click **Delete project**.

This permanently deletes the project and everything in it, including its databases, branches, deployments, and Compute resources.

Deletion is refused when:

- The project is in a workspace managed through the Vercel Marketplace.
- The project has resources managed by Netlify or Stripe Projects. Remove them from that service first.

## Delete a database

1. Open the database and go to its **Settings** tab.
2. Click **Delete Database** and confirm.

You can't delete a project's default database. To delete it, open another database in the same project, turn on **Default database** in its **Settings** tab and save, and then delete the old one. To remove the last database, [delete the project](#delete-a-project) instead.

A database created through the Vercel Marketplace is deleted from your Vercel project dashboard, not from the Console. See [How do I delete a Prisma Postgres database I created through Vercel?](/postgres/faq#how-do-i-delete-a-prisma-postgres-database-i-created-through-vercel).

## What deletion removes

Deleting a database also deletes its connection strings. Deleting a project deletes every database in it and removes any Prisma Compute apps in the project. Deleting a workspace deletes all of its projects and their databases. The Console removes them straight away. The databases themselves are then deleted in the background, so this can finish shortly after the workspace disappears from the Console.

Invoices and payment records are kept by our payment processor after deletion, so past invoices remain available for accounting. Backups and logs of a deleted database aren't kept after deletion. For a formal data-erasure request under GDPR, email [dpo@prisma.io](mailto:dpo@prisma.io).

## Can a deletion be undone?

Deletion is permanent. Prisma can't restore a deleted account, workspace, project, or database, from the Console or through support, and a database's backups are deleted with it. Export anything you want to keep before you delete it (see [Before you delete: export your data](#before-you-delete-export-your-data)).

## Before you delete: export your data

Deleting a database deletes its data. To keep a copy, create a backup file with `pg_dump` over a direct connection before you delete anything. See [Export your data](/postgres/database/export-data) for the steps.

If your Free workspace is blocked because it reached its plan limit, the database doesn't accept queries, so an export fails. See [Usage limits and blocks](/console/billing/usage-limits-and-blocks) for how to regain access long enough to export.

## Data erasure requests

There is no separate data-erasure process in the Console. Deleting your account removes your user and the workspaces where you're the only member. For a formal GDPR erasure or other data subject request, email [dpo@prisma.io](mailto:dpo@prisma.io). See also [Security and compliance](/console/more/security-and-compliance#gdpr-and-data-subject-requests).

## Common questions

### How do I delete my Prisma account?

Click your name at the bottom of the Console sidebar, choose **Account settings**, and click **Delete Account**. You don't need to email support. If deletion is refused, the Console names the workspace and the cause; see [If account deletion is blocked](#if-account-deletion-is-blocked).

### Do I still need to email support to delete my account?

No. Account deletion is self-service. You do still need to disable Accelerate on databases in workspaces where you're the only member, because enabled Accelerate blocks deletion. See [If account deletion is blocked](#if-account-deletion-is-blocked).

### Why can't I delete my workspace?

It's either your last workspace, on a paid plan (including a cancelled plan that hasn't reached the end of its billing period), has an unpaid invoice, has active Prisma Compute deployments, or is managed through an integration. Clear the blocker and try again.

### If I delete my account, does my team lose our shared workspace?

No. Workspaces with other members are kept. Only the workspaces where you're the only member are deleted.

### Why can't I delete this project?

The workspace is managed through the Vercel Marketplace, or the project has resources managed by Netlify or Stripe Projects. Remove those resources from that service first.

### Why can't I delete this database?

It's the project's default database, or it was created through the Vercel Marketplace. Make another database the default first, or delete the whole project. For Vercel databases, delete them in Vercel.

### Can I get my database back after deleting it?

No. Deletion is permanent, and neither the Console nor Prisma support can restore a deleted database, because its backups are deleted with it. Export your data with `pg_dump` before you delete it.
