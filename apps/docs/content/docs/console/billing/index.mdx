Each Prisma workspace has its own plan and its own invoices. This page explains when you're charged, what each invoice contains, and where to look in [Prisma Console](https://console.prisma.io) when a charge doesn't match what you expected.

## Billing is per workspace

A [workspace](/console/concepts#workspace) is the unit Prisma bills. The plan, the payment method, the billing email, and the invoices all belong to the workspace, and one invoice covers every project, Prisma Postgres database, and Prisma Compute app in it.

If you belong to several workspaces, each one has its own plan. Upgrading one workspace does not change the plan or the limits of another, and cancelling one does not cancel another. When you see a charge you don't recognise, check the **Billing** page of every workspace you belong to, using the workspace switcher in Console.

## The billing cycle

Each billing period lasts one month and renews on the same day every month, at 00:00 UTC. For a new paid subscription, that day is the day you upgraded: a workspace that upgrades on April 17 renews on May 17, June 17, and so on. Paying an invoice late does not move the renewal day.

Subscriptions created before Prisma switched to signup-day renewals keep renewing on the 1st of each month until they're cancelled. If you upgrade on the 29th, 30th, or 31st, a shorter month renews on its last day, and the subscription stays on that day from then on.

To see the renewal day for your workspace, open **Workspace Settings > Billing** and look at the **Subscription** card, which shows when the current billing period renews. **View Invoices** on the same page opens your invoice list, and each invoice shows the period it covers.

Billing periods follow UTC, so depending on your time zone, the new period and its invoice can land on the evening before the renewal day in your local time.

## What you pay in advance and what you pay in arrears

A paid plan has two kinds of charges, and they're billed at different times:

- **The plan fee is charged in advance.** At the start of each billing period you pay the plan fee for the period that is starting.
- **Usage is charged in arrears.** Usage above what your plan includes is measured during the billing period and billed after the period ends. This covers Prisma Postgres operations, Prisma Postgres storage, and Prisma Compute usage (requests, provisioned memory, active CPU, and outbound bandwidth).

So a regular invoice usually has two parts: the plan fee for the new period and any usage charges for the period that just ended. For example, a Starter workspace that renews on the 17th gets an invoice on May 17 with the Starter fee for May 17 to June 16 and the charge for operations above the Starter allowance used from April 17 to May 16.

Because usage is billed after the fact, you receive one final usage invoice after you cancel a paid plan. See [Change or cancel your plan](/console/billing/change-or-cancel-plan) for the timing.

The Free plan has no plan fee and no usage charges. When a Free workspace uses up its monthly allowance, its databases are blocked instead of billed. See [Usage limits and blocks](/console/billing/usage-limits-and-blocks).

For the plan fees, included usage, and per-unit rates, see the [pricing page](https://www.prisma.io/pricing).

## Currency

Prisma bills in US dollars (USD) only. Every price and every invoice is in USD, and a subscription can't be switched to another currency.

If your card statement shows a charge in your local currency, your card issuer converted the USD charge. The issuer sets the exchange rate and any foreign transaction fee.

## Where to find billing information in Console

Billing settings are under **Workspace Settings > Billing** in [Prisma Console](https://console.prisma.io). From there you can:

- see the workspace's current plan and change it with **Manage subscription** (a Free workspace sees **Upgrade** instead)
- update the billing email and other billing details with **Manage billing information**
- manage payment methods with **Manage payment methods**
- view and download past invoices with **View Invoices**
- set a [spend limit](/console/billing/spend-limits)

Invoices, payment methods, billing email, tax IDs, and failed payments are covered in [Invoices and payments](/console/billing/invoices-and-payments).

## Check usage and your next invoice

To check usage and your next invoice before it's issued, open **Usage** in the workspace sidebar. With **Usage period** set to **Current month**, the **Current Invoice** card shows the estimated **Invoice total** for the workspace so far, the date it renews, and a breakdown of the plan fee and any usage charges. Below it, the page charts app and database usage. Switch to **Last month** to see the previous month's invoice.

The estimate updates every few hours, so very recent usage may not be in it yet. Accelerate usage for databases hosted outside Prisma isn't included; check it from the project instead.

The **Subscription** card under **Workspace Settings > Billing** also shows how many of your plan's included Prisma Postgres operations you've used this billing period and when they reset. For a single Prisma Postgres database, the [`postgres usage`](/cli/postgres) CLI command prints its usage metrics.

## Workspaces created through the Vercel Marketplace

If you created Prisma Postgres from the Vercel dashboard, the integration created a separate Prisma workspace for that Vercel installation, and Vercel bills it. The same plans and rates apply, but your Prisma usage appears on your Vercel invoice, Vercel collects the payment, and the billing email follows your Vercel team's contact. That workspace is separate from any workspace you created directly in Prisma Console, so it's possible to have one plan billed by Prisma and another billed by Vercel. See [Vercel Marketplace billing](/console/billing/vercel-marketplace) for the differences.

## Common questions

### Why was I charged after I cancelled?

Cancellation takes effect at the end of the current billing period, and usage is billed in arrears. After the plan ends you receive one more invoice for the usage above your plan's allowance during the last period. If the charge is for a later period, check whether another workspace you belong to is still on a paid plan.

### Why does my invoice include last month's usage and next month's plan fee?

The plan fee is charged in advance for the billing period that is starting, and usage is charged in arrears for the period that just ended. Both land on the same invoice, issued on your renewal day.

### Can I change my billing date?

No. A subscription renews at 00:00 UTC on the day it started, and the date can't be moved. The **Subscription** card under **Workspace Settings > Billing** shows when the current period renews.

### How can I see what my next invoice will be?

Open **Usage** in the workspace sidebar. With **Usage period** set to **Current month**, the **Current Invoice** card shows the estimated total so far, updated every few hours.

### Why is my card charged in a different currency?

Prisma charges in USD only, so your card issuer converts the charge into your card's currency and sets the rate and any fees.

### I upgraded a workspace but my other workspace is still blocked. Why?

Plans are per workspace. Upgrading one workspace changes only that workspace, so upgrade the workspace that holds the blocked database.

### I'm paying twice for Prisma Postgres. Why?

You probably have two workspaces on paid plans, often one created in Prisma Console and one created through the Vercel Marketplace. Decide which workspace holds the data you use and cancel the plan on the other one.
