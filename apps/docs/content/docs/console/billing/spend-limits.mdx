A spend limit is a cap you set on how much usage a paid workspace can run up in a billing month. When the workspace's usage charges reach the cap, Prisma blocks your Prisma Postgres databases until you raise or remove the limit, so a traffic spike or a runaway query loop can't keep running up database charges. Prisma Compute apps aren't stopped by a spend limit; see [What counts toward the limit](#what-counts-toward-the-limit).

Spend limits are available on every paid plan, including workspaces created through the [Vercel Marketplace](/console/billing/vercel-marketplace). The Free plan doesn't have a spend limit because it has a fixed monthly usage allowance instead; see [Usage limits and blocks](/console/billing/usage-limits-and-blocks#free-plan-usage-limit).

## Set or change a spend limit

1. Open the [Prisma Console](https://console.prisma.io) and select the workspace.
2. Go to **Workspace Settings > Billing**.
3. In the **Spending Limit** card, enter the amount in the **Spending limit** field and select **Set Limit**.
4. Type the workspace name in the dialog to confirm.

To remove the limit, select **Clear Limit** in the same card and type the workspace name to confirm.

The limit applies to the whole workspace, not to a single project or database. If you belong to several workspaces, each one has its own limit.

New paid workspaces have no spend limit. Usage charges are uncapped until someone in the workspace sets one, so set a limit after upgrading if you want that protection.

## What counts toward the limit

The limit counts the usage charges that accrue on top of your plan fee during the current billing month. That's every usage charge in the workspace: Prisma Postgres operations and storage, Accelerate, and Prisma Compute requests, memory, CPU, and bandwidth. The fixed monthly plan fee doesn't count.

Whether tax counts depends on who bills the workspace:

- **Workspaces billed by Prisma:** the limit counts usage charges including any tax charged on your invoice, and any account credit you hold reduces the amount counted.
- **Workspaces billed through the Vercel Marketplace:** the limit counts usage charges before tax, because Vercel adds tax when it bills you.

When the limit is reached, the block stops your Prisma Postgres databases. Prisma Compute apps keep running, and their usage is still billed, so Compute charges can go past the limit.

For what each plan includes before usage charges start, see the [pricing page](https://www.prisma.io/pricing).

## Warning and block emails

Prisma sends two emails as you approach and reach the limit:

| When                                    | Email subject                                                          |
| --------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Usage charges reach 75% of the limit    | Action Required: Update Limits Now to Maintain Uninterrupted Service   |
| Usage charges reach 100% of the limit   | Action Required: Update Limits Now to Restore Service                  |

A workspace with no spend limit gets neither email and is never blocked for spending. All of its usage is billed.

Both emails go to every member of the workspace, not to the billing email, so a billing contact who isn't a member doesn't receive them. Each member gets each email at most once per billing month. The 75% warning threshold is fixed and can't be changed.

## How often the limit is checked

Prisma checks usage against the limit once an hour. Because of that delay, a sudden spike can take usage charges slightly past the limit before the block lands. Usage above the limit is still billed.

A spend limit only stops future usage. It doesn't reduce charges that have already accrued, and it doesn't cap an invoice after the fact.

## What happens when you reach the limit

When usage charges reach the limit, the workspace is blocked:

- Your Prisma Postgres databases in the workspace stop accepting queries. Database clients can return an error that mentions `spendLimitReached`.
- Prisma Compute apps keep running, and their usage is still billed.
- The Console shows the banner "Usage blocked: your workspace has reached its spending limit."
- The database list shows the tooltip "Usage blocked: Spending limit reached. Increase limit to continue."

No data is deleted. See [Usage limits and blocks](/console/billing/usage-limits-and-blocks) for what keeps working during a block and how the three kinds of block differ.

## Lift a spend-limit block

Raise the limit with **Set Limit**, or remove it with **Clear Limit**, under **Workspace Settings > Billing**. Either way you confirm by typing the workspace name. The block lifts as soon as you confirm, and you don't need to contact support.

Set the new limit **above** the usage charges the workspace has already accrued this month. Prisma re-checks the new limit straight away, and if it's at or below current usage charges, including exactly equal to them, the workspace is blocked again within minutes. Check the current usage on the billing page before you choose the new amount.

Paying an invoice doesn't lift a spend-limit block. Only changing the limit does.

If you don't change the limit, the block lifts on its own when the next billing month starts, at 00:00 UTC on the 1st. The limit stays set and applies again to the new month's usage charges, which start from zero.

If you've raised the limit above current usage and queries still fail, or the Console banner is gone but your database client still returns `spendLimitReached`, [contact support](/console/more/support) with the workspace name and the error text.

## Spend limits on Vercel Marketplace workspaces

Spend limits work the same way on workspaces created through the Vercel Marketplace. You set the limit in the Prisma Console under **Workspace Settings > Billing**, not in Vercel.

The limit is measured against Prisma usage charges before tax. Vercel adds tax to the invoice it sends you, so the amount on your Vercel invoice can be higher than the usage the limit counts.

If you paid your Vercel invoice and the workspace still shows a spend-limit block, the payment isn't the issue: raise the limit in Console. See [Vercel Marketplace billing](/console/billing/vercel-marketplace).

## Common questions

### How do I stop my bill from going over a certain amount?

Set a spend limit under **Workspace Settings > Billing** in the Console. Your Prisma Postgres databases are blocked once usage charges reach the limit, though the hourly check means a spike can push charges slightly past it. The plan fee is billed on top of the limit. Prisma Compute apps keep running past the limit, so their charges aren't capped.

### Why is my database blocked with `spendLimitReached`?

Your workspace's usage charges for this month reached the spend limit you set. Raise or remove the limit in **Workspace Settings > Billing** and the block lifts immediately. If you leave it, the block lifts at 00:00 UTC on the 1st of next month.

### I raised my spend limit but got blocked again. Why?

The new limit was at or below the usage charges you'd already accrued this month, so the next check re-blocked the workspace. Set the limit above your current usage charges.

### Does the spend limit include my plan fee?

No. It only counts usage charges on top of the plan fee. For workspaces billed by Prisma, tax on your invoice does count toward the limit. For Vercel Marketplace workspaces, the limit counts usage before tax.

### I paid my invoice. Why am I still blocked?

A spend-limit block isn't tied to payment, so paying doesn't lift it. Raise the limit. If your block is for an unpaid invoice instead, see [Usage limits and blocks](/console/billing/usage-limits-and-blocks#unpaid-invoice-block).

### Is a spend limit set by default?

No. A new paid workspace has no limit until someone in the workspace sets one.

### Does a spend limit stop my Prisma Compute apps?

No. Compute usage counts toward the limit, but reaching it only blocks Prisma Postgres databases. Compute apps keep running and billing.

### Who gets the spend-limit emails?

Every member of the workspace. The billing email doesn't receive them unless it belongs to a member.

### Can I set a spend limit on the Free plan?

No. The Free plan has a fixed monthly allowance instead and is blocked when it runs out. See [Usage limits and blocks](/console/billing/usage-limits-and-blocks#free-plan-usage-limit).
