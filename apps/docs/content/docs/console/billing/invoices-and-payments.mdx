Each Prisma workspace is billed on its own, with its own plan, payment method, billing email, and invoices. If you belong to several workspaces, check each one when you're looking for a charge. Stripe processes card payments for Prisma.

Workspaces created through the Vercel Marketplace are billed by Vercel, not Prisma. If that's you, most of this page doesn't apply; see [Vercel Marketplace billing](/console/billing/vercel-marketplace).

For how a monthly invoice is built (the plan fee charged in advance, usage charged after the month ends), see [How billing works](/console/billing).

## Find your invoices

Billing for a workspace is under **Workspace Settings > Billing** in the [Prisma Console](https://console.prisma.io). To see past invoices, select **View Invoices** in the **Invoice History** card. This opens the billing portal, run by Stripe, where you can see every invoice for the workspace and download invoices and receipts as PDFs.

Invoices and receipts are emailed to the workspace's billing email. For the workspace created when you signed up, that's your sign-up email until you change it.

## Change the billing email

Invoices and payment-failure emails go to the workspace's billing email. To change it:

1. Go to **Workspace Settings > Billing**.
2. Select **Manage billing information** and update the email.

The billing email is separate from the email you sign in with. Removing a member from the workspace, or changing your own sign-in email, doesn't change it, so invoices keep going to the old address until you update it here. See [Change your account email](/console/account/change-email).

For Vercel Marketplace workspaces, the billing email follows your Vercel team's contact and is changed in Vercel.

## Payment methods

Prisma accepts payment by card. Depending on your country, checkout may also offer other methods supported by Stripe. Prisma doesn't offer bank transfer or manual invoicing on self-serve plans.

To add, replace, or remove a payment method, go to **Workspace Settings > Billing** and select **Manage payment methods** in the **Payment Methods** card. This opens the billing portal, where you can add a new card, make it the default, and remove old ones.

## Tax IDs and company details on invoices

You can add these yourself. Go to **Workspace Settings > Billing** and select **Manage billing information** in the **Billing Information** card. In the billing portal that opens, you can set the company or customer name, the billing address, and a VAT number or other tax ID. You also enter a billing address, and optionally a tax ID, at checkout when you first upgrade.

Changes apply to invoices issued from then on. Invoices that were already issued keep the details they had. If you need an earlier invoice corrected, [contact support](/console/more/support) with the invoice number.

## Tax residency certificates and tax forms

Prisma doesn't publish tax documents such as a tax residency certificate, a W-8, or a W-9 in the Console. To request one, [contact support](/console/more/support) from the email on your Prisma account, and say which document and which tax year you need. Support passes the request to Prisma's finance team. These documents are issued by the tax authority, so they can take some time.

## Currency

Prisma bills in US dollars only. Every price and every invoice is in USD, and you can't switch a subscription to another currency.

If your card statement shows a different currency, your card issuer has converted the USD charge. The issuer sets the exchange rate and any foreign transaction fee.

## When a payment fails

A failed payment doesn't block your workspace straight away. This is the timeline:

1. **The payment fails.** The Console shows "Your last invoice was not paid. Pay your outstanding balance to avoid this Workspace being blocked." You get an email with the subject "Action Required: Payment Required to Maintain Uninterrupted Service".
2. **Further attempts.** The payment is retried, and you get the same email after each failed attempt.
3. **The workspace is blocked**, 15 days after a failed payment, at 16:00 UTC. A block date that falls on a Friday, Saturday, or Sunday moves to the following Monday. You get an email with the subject "Action Required: Payment Required to Restore Service", the Console shows "Your Workspace is blocked due to an unpaid invoice. Pay your outstanding balance to unlock this Workspace.", and your databases stop accepting queries.
4. **The payment succeeds.** The block lifts automatically and you get an email with the subject "Your payment has been received".

Stripe retries a failed payment automatically over the following days. You don't have to wait for the next retry: after you update your card, select **Pay your outstanding balance** in the Console banner, or use the payment link in the email, and pay the open invoice straight away.

:::warning[TODO(support-docs)]
**Verify:** The 15 days may count from the first failed payment or restart from each later failed attempt, and the number and timing of automatic retries aren't confirmed; check both against the live billing settings.
:::

If your bank declined the payment, contact your bank or update your payment method, then pay the open invoice. Paying late doesn't move your billing month: billing periods follow the calendar month, so a new month's invoice can arrive soon after you pay an overdue one.

While the workspace is blocked for an unpaid invoice, you can't change its plan or delete it. See [Usage limits and blocks](/console/billing/usage-limits-and-blocks#unpaid-invoice-block) for what the block stops.

## A payment that's still processing

Some payment methods take several days to settle. Until the payment succeeds, the invoice still counts as unpaid, so a blocked workspace stays blocked even if you see the invoice as paid. If your workspace is blocked while a payment settles, [contact support](/console/more/support) with the invoice number, and support can remove the block in the meantime.

A processing payment can still fail. If it does, the invoice is open again and the workspace will be blocked again unless you pay it.

## "This invoice can no longer be paid"

After repeated failed payments, Stripe can close an invoice so it can't be paid any more. The payment page then shows "This invoice can no longer be paid on Stripe. Please contact Prisma Data, Inc."

To fix it:

1. [Contact support](/console/more/support) with the invoice number.
2. Support sends you a replacement invoice with a new payment link.
3. Pay the replacement invoice.
4. Reply to support to say you've paid.

Paying the replacement invoice doesn't lift the block by itself, because the block is tied to the original invoice. Support lifts it once you confirm the payment.

:::danger
Prisma support never asks you to pay a third party, send cryptocurrency, or "activate a wallet". Replacement invoices come from Prisma through Stripe. If anyone asks you to pay another way, they are not Prisma.
:::

## Common questions

### Where can I download my invoices?

Go to **Workspace Settings > Billing** in the Console and select **View Invoices**. The billing portal that opens lists every invoice for that workspace, with PDF downloads of invoices and receipts. Each workspace has its own invoices. If you're billed through Vercel, your invoices are in Vercel.

### How do I change the email my invoices go to?

Go to **Workspace Settings > Billing**, select **Manage billing information**, and change the email there. Changing your sign-in email doesn't change it.

### Can I add my VAT number or company name to my invoices?

Yes. Select **Manage billing information** under **Workspace Settings > Billing**. Changes apply to invoices issued from then on, not to ones already issued.

### Can I pay by bank transfer?

No. Self-serve plans are paid by card, or another method checkout offers in your country. Bank transfer and manual invoicing aren't available.

### Why was I charged in my local currency?

You weren't: Prisma charges in USD only. Your card issuer converted the charge and set the exchange rate and any fee.

### Can I get a tax residency certificate or a W-9?

Contact support from your account email with the document and tax year you need, and support passes the request to Prisma's finance team. It can take some time because the tax authority issues these documents.

### My payment failed. When will my database stop working?

The workspace is blocked about 15 days after the payment failed, at 16:00 UTC (moved to the following Monday if that day is a Friday, Saturday, or Sunday). Pay the open invoice before then to avoid the block.

### Stripe says my invoice can no longer be paid. What do I do?

Contact support with the invoice number. Support sends a replacement invoice; pay it, then reply so support can lift the block.

### I paid but my workspace is still blocked. Why?

The payment may still be processing, or you paid a replacement invoice, and neither lifts the block automatically. Contact support with the invoice number. If the Console message mentions a spending limit, raise your [spend limit](/console/billing/spend-limits) instead.

### I'm being charged but I don't use Prisma. Why?

Billing is per workspace, so check every workspace you belong to for a paid plan, including any created through Vercel. Usage is also billed after the month ends, so you can get one more invoice after you cancel. See [Change or cancel your plan](/console/billing/change-or-cancel-plan).
