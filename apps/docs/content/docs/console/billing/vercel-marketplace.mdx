When you create a Prisma Postgres database from the Vercel dashboard, Vercel bills you for it and some account actions happen in Vercel instead of the Prisma Console. This page covers how that differs from a workspace you create directly at Prisma. To set up the integration, see [Prisma Postgres on Vercel](/guides/postgres/vercel).

## How to tell if your workspace is billed through Vercel

A Vercel Marketplace workspace was created from the Vercel dashboard, is named after the Vercel team that installed the integration, and appears on your Vercel invoices. If you can't find a Prisma receipt for a charge, check your Vercel invoices.

## One Prisma workspace per installation

Each time a Vercel team installs the Prisma integration, Prisma creates a separate workspace for that installation. It's separate from any workspace you created directly in the Prisma Console, even when you use the same email for both.

That means you can have two workspaces on two plans: one billed by Prisma, and one billed by Vercel. This is the usual cause of "I'm paying twice". To fix it:

1. Find out which workspace holds the databases you use. Both appear in the workspace switcher in the Console.
2. Cancel the paid plan on the other workspace. Cancelling one workspace's plan never cancels the other.

A Vercel-billed workspace can't also have a Prisma-billed subscription, so double billing always means two workspaces.

## Who bills you

For a Vercel Marketplace workspace, Vercel collects payment. Prisma doesn't hold a payment method for you and can't charge or retry your card. You pay, and dispute charges, on your Vercel invoice.

The plans and rates are the same as for any Prisma workspace. See the [pricing page](https://www.prisma.io/pricing).

### Don't pay a Prisma invoice directly

Pay only your Vercel invoice. If you see a Prisma invoice for a Vercel-billed workspace and pay it as well, you've paid twice for the same usage. If that happens, [contact support](/console/more/support) with both invoice numbers.

### Why the Vercel charge differs from Prisma's usage amount

The Console shows your Prisma usage charges before tax. Vercel adds tax when it bills you, so your Vercel invoice can be higher than the amount the Console shows. The tax is charged by Vercel.

### Billing email

The billing email for a Vercel Marketplace workspace follows your Vercel team's contact details. You change it in Vercel, not in the Console.

## Refunds

Money for a Vercel Marketplace workspace is collected by Vercel, so any refund is paid back through Vercel's billing, not to a card on file with Prisma.

For refund questions about Prisma charges on your Vercel invoice, contact [Prisma support](/console/more/support), not Vercel. Prisma reviews the request.

## Change your plan

You can change the plan in either Vercel or the Console, and the change is reflected in both.

To change it in Vercel:

1. Open your [Vercel](https://vercel.com/) dashboard and switch to the team that installed the integration.
2. Go to the **Integrations** tab and select **Manage** on the Prisma integration.
3. Open the **Settings** tab.
4. Under **Current Installation Plan Level**, select **Change Plan** and choose the new plan.

To change it in the Console, a workspace member can go to **Workspace Settings > Billing**.

Upgrades apply immediately. Downgrades and cancellations take effect at the end of the billing period, and you keep the current plan until then. An upgrade lifts a Free plan usage-limit block automatically. If you upgraded and the database is still blocked, check the plan shown in Vercel; if it shows the paid plan, contact support with the project name and a screenshot of the block message.

If the workspace has an unpaid Vercel invoice, you can't change its plan until that invoice is paid.

## Spend limits

Spend limits work on Vercel Marketplace workspaces. You set them in the Prisma Console under **Workspace Settings > Billing**, not in Vercel. The limit counts usage charges before tax, so the tax Vercel adds isn't included. Reaching the limit blocks your Prisma Postgres databases; Prisma Compute apps keep running and billing.

If your database is blocked with a spend-limit message after you've paid your Vercel invoice, paying isn't what lifts it: raise the limit above your current usage charges. See [Spend limits](/console/billing/spend-limits).

## Unpaid Vercel invoices

When a Vercel invoice isn't paid, Vercel shows "Your invoice is unpaid. Please resolve within 15 days to avoid service interruption." If it's still unpaid 15 days after the invoice date, the Prisma workspace is blocked. The block lifts when Vercel reports the invoice as paid. See [Usage limits and blocks](/console/billing/usage-limits-and-blocks#unpaid-invoice-block).

## Finding your databases

In Vercel, a Prisma Postgres database appears only under the Vercel team or personal account that installed the integration. If you can't find it, use the team switcher in Vercel and check the **Storage** tab under each team.

**Open in Prisma** in Vercel signs you in to the Prisma Console. Sign-in matches by email, so if you also sign in to the Console directly with the same email, the Vercel workspace is in your workspace switcher.

## Credentials and Rotate Secrets

The default connection string for a Vercel database is created by Vercel. The Console labels it as "created by Vercel and cannot be managed or removed from this view".

**Rotate Secrets** in Vercel creates new credentials and gives your Vercel project new values for the environment variables the integration manages, such as `DATABASE_URL`. It replaces only the credentials Vercel created. Connection strings you created yourself in the Console aren't changed and stay valid until you delete them in the Console. Redeploy your Vercel project after rotating so it uses the new values. The step-by-step flow is in [Rotating your Prisma integration secrets in Vercel](/guides/postgres/vercel#rotating-your-prisma-integration-secrets-in-vercel).

With a delay period of `0`, the old Vercel credentials are deleted straight away. With a delay, they're deleted when the delay ends. If you need to be certain every old credential is revoked, for example after a rotation that reported an error, create new connection strings in the Console and delete all the old ones. See [Database roles and credentials](/postgres/database/roles-and-credentials).

## Deleting databases and uninstalling

A Vercel Marketplace workspace, and the projects and databases in it, can't be deleted from the Prisma Console. Delete databases from the Vercel dashboard instead.

If your only Prisma workspace is managed by Vercel, you can't delete your Prisma account from the Console until the integration is removed in Vercel. See [Delete your account](/console/account/delete-account).

### Uninstalling the integration

You can only uninstall the integration while the workspace is on the Free plan. If it's on a paid plan, Vercel refuses the uninstall with an error asking you to downgrade first ("Downgrade to continue"). Uninstalling is also refused while an invoice is unpaid or while Prisma Compute apps are still deployed.

When you uninstall a Free workspace's integration, Prisma deletes the workspace straight away, including every project and Prisma Postgres database in it and all their data. There's no grace period and the data can't be recovered, so export anything you need first. Uninstalling doesn't create an extra invoice. Usage from your last paid billing period is billed through Vercel as usual.

## Common questions

### Why am I paying both Prisma and Vercel?

You most likely have two Prisma workspaces: one you created at Prisma and one created by the Vercel integration, each with its own plan. Find the one holding the databases you use and cancel the plan on the other.

### Why is my Vercel charge higher than the usage shown in Prisma?

The Console shows usage before tax, and Vercel adds tax to the invoice it sends you.

### I paid a Prisma invoice but I'm billed through Vercel. What do I do?

Pay only through Vercel. Contact Prisma support with both invoice numbers so the duplicate payment can be sorted out.

### Where do I get a refund for a Vercel Marketplace charge?

Contact Prisma support with the invoice details. Prisma reviews refund requests for Prisma charges, and any refund is paid back through Vercel's billing because Vercel collected the payment.

### I can't find the Prisma integration in Vercel. Where is it?

It only appears under the Vercel team that installed it. Use the team switcher and check the **Storage** tab under each team.

### I upgraded in Vercel but my database is still blocked. Why?

An upgrade normally lifts the Free plan block within a minute. Check that Vercel shows the paid plan, and if it does, contact support. If the message mentions a spending limit, raise the limit in the Console instead.

### Will rotating secrets in Vercel break connection strings I made in the Console?

No. Rotate Secrets replaces only the credentials Vercel created. Connection strings you made in the Console keep working until you delete them.

### What happens if I uninstall the Prisma integration in Vercel?

On the Free plan, the Prisma workspace and all its databases are deleted immediately, with no grace period. On a paid plan, the uninstall is refused until you downgrade to Free. It's also refused while an invoice is unpaid or Prisma Compute apps are deployed.

### How do I delete a database created through Vercel?

Delete it from the Vercel dashboard. The Console can't delete databases, projects, or workspaces that Vercel manages.
