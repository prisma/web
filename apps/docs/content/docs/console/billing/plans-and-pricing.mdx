Every workspace is on one plan, and the plan sets the monthly fee, how much Prisma Postgres and Prisma Compute usage is included, and the rate for usage beyond that.

For each plan's monthly fee, included usage, per-unit rates, and limits, see the [pricing page](https://www.prisma.io/pricing). This page explains how those plans work.

## Prisma Postgres plans

New subscriptions are on the Free, Starter, Pro, or Business plan. For each plan's fee, included operations and storage, overage rates, databases per workspace, and backup retention, see the [pricing page](https://www.prisma.io/pricing).

Storage is measured in GiB (1 GiB = 1,073,741,824 bytes). Prisma meters storage per hour, so included storage and storage overage are based on the average amount stored across the month. For example, 20 GiB stored for half the month counts as about 10 GiB.

Included operations and storage are pooled across every database in the workspace. They're not counted per database. Prisma Postgres does not charge for egress (data transfer).

Paid plans are billed as a monthly fee in advance plus usage above the allowance in arrears. See [How billing works](/console/billing) for the timing.

For backup details, see [Backups](/postgres/database/backups). If you need more databases in a workspace than your plan allows, contact [support@prisma.io](mailto:support@prisma.io): that limit isn't fixed, and Prisma will set up a call to discuss your use case.

### Enterprise

Above Business, Prisma offers an Enterprise plan on a custom contract. It starts from the Business plan and is tailored to your needs, with dedicated support and a dedicated contact, help with security reviews, procurement and compliance, and custom limits. It isn't available as a self-serve upgrade in Console. To discuss it, use the contact form on the [Enterprise page](https://www.prisma.io/enterprise).

### Support by plan

For the support tier each plan includes, see the [pricing page](https://www.prisma.io/pricing).

On any plan, including Free and Starter, you can email [support@prisma.io](mailto:support@prisma.io) about billing questions. See [Support](/console/more/support) for channels and response times on each plan.

## What counts as an operation

An operation is one request to your database. A read or a write, simple or complex, counts as one operation, and query execution time does not affect the price. How a request is counted depends on how you connect:

- With Prisma ORM, each Prisma Client query is one operation, even when it runs several SQL statements.
- Over a direct connection, each SQL statement is one operation. This includes health checks such as `SELECT 1`.

The [Prisma Postgres FAQ](/postgres/faq#what-is-an-operation) covers operations in more depth, including how transactions are counted, how to reduce your operation count, and worked cost estimates for each plan.

## Free plan limits

The Free plan has no usage charges. Instead it has hard monthly limits on Prisma Postgres operations (pooled across every database in the workspace), storage, and databases per workspace, plus its own Prisma Compute limits. For the current values, see the [pricing page](https://www.prisma.io/pricing).

When a Free workspace uses all its operations, its databases stop accepting queries until you upgrade to a paid plan or the next calendar month starts. Upgrading lifts the block, usually within a minute. See [Usage limits and blocks](/console/billing/usage-limits-and-blocks).

The Free plan is intended for evaluation, not for production workloads.

## Earlier plan versions

Prisma has changed its plans over time. A workspace that subscribed under an earlier plan version may still be on it. The earlier versions included 100,000 operations per month, then charged $18 per million on Starter, $8 per million on Pro, and $6 per million on Business, with 1 GiB, 5 GiB, and 10 GiB of included storage respectively.

Console doesn't label plan versions. To see which rates apply to your workspace, check the per-unit rates on a recent invoice or in the **Current Invoice** breakdown on the **Usage** page.

## Prisma Compute pricing

Prisma Compute is billed on the same workspace plan. Each plan includes a monthly request allowance, and paid plans bill additional requests, provisioned memory, active CPU, and outbound bandwidth as usage in arrears. The Free plan has monthly limits on all four and no usage billing. For the rates and the included usage per plan, see the [pricing page](https://www.prisma.io/pricing). [Prisma Compute pricing](/compute/pricing) explains how Compute usage is metered.

An app that stays running holds its provisioned memory, and you pay for that memory even when it serves few requests. An idle app that has scaled to zero costs nothing.

## Accelerate with your own database

The operations included in a plan apply to Prisma Postgres. Using Accelerate in front of a database you host elsewhere is metered separately, with its own monthly allowance. On the Free plan, Accelerate operations beyond the allowance are blocked until you upgrade or the next month starts; on paid plans they're billed per operation. For the Accelerate allowance and rates, see the [pricing page](https://www.prisma.io/pricing).

The Accelerate allowance is separate from your Prisma Postgres operations, so you can reach the Accelerate limit while most of your Prisma Postgres allowance is unused. The workspace **Usage** page doesn't include Accelerate usage for databases hosted elsewhere; open the project to see it. The pricing page also lists Accelerate egress and cache invalidation limits.

## Common questions

### Are a plan's included operations per database?

No. Included operations are pooled across every database in the workspace, so all databases share one allowance.

### Does a `SELECT 1` health check count as an operation?

Yes. Over a direct connection, every executed statement counts as one operation, including `SELECT 1`.

### Why is my rate different from the pricing page?

Your workspace may be on an earlier plan version with different rates. Check your invoice, or the **Current Invoice** breakdown on the **Usage** page, for the rate applied, and compare it with the [earlier plan versions](#earlier-plan-versions) listed on this page.

### Is storage billed in GB or GiB?

GiB. Storage is metered per hour and billed on the average amount stored across the month.

### Is there a plan above Business?

Yes. The Enterprise plan is a custom contract. Use the contact form on the [Enterprise page](https://www.prisma.io/enterprise).

### What happens when I go over my plan's included operations?

On a paid plan, operations above the allowance are billed at your plan's per-million rate on the next invoice. On the Free plan, your databases are blocked until you upgrade or the next month starts. To cap usage charges on a paid plan, set a [spend limit](/console/billing/spend-limits).

### Which plan is cheapest for high usage?

Compare the plan fee plus overage for your expected monthly operations, using the rates on the [pricing page](https://www.prisma.io/pricing). At high volume, a plan with a higher fee and a lower per-million rate can cost less in total. The [Prisma Postgres FAQ](/postgres/faq) has the formula.

### Do Prisma Postgres and Prisma Compute need separate plans?

No. The workspace plan covers both. Each product has its own included usage and rates, listed on the [pricing page](https://www.prisma.io/pricing).
