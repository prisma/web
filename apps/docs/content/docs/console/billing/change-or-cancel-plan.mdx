You change a workspace's plan in [Prisma Console](https://console.prisma.io) under **Workspace Settings > Billing**. Upgrades apply straight away, while downgrades and cancellations wait until the end of the current billing period, so the timing of the change decides what you pay.

Plans are per workspace. Changing the plan of one workspace does not affect any other workspace you belong to.

## Who can change the plan

Any member of the workspace can change its plan from **Workspace Settings > Billing**. Workspaces have no separate owner or billing role. See [Members](/console/workspaces/members).

A workspace that has an unpaid invoice, or is blocked because of one, can't change plan until the invoice is paid. This also means a Free workspace in that state can't upgrade out of a plan-limit block. See [Invoices and payments](/console/billing/invoices-and-payments).

## Upgrade your plan

1. Open **Workspace Settings > Billing** for the workspace you want to upgrade.
2. Select **Upgrade** if the workspace is on the Free plan, or **Manage subscription** if it's already on a paid plan, and choose the plan you want.
3. If the workspace is on the Free plan, enter your payment details in the checkout page that opens.

The new plan applies immediately. If the workspace was blocked for reaching the Free plan's monthly limit, the block lifts on its own within about a minute, and you don't need to do anything else.

### How upgrades are charged

- **From Free to a paid plan:** your first invoice charges the plan fee for the rest of the current month only (prorated). From the 1st of the next month you pay the full fee in advance.
- **From one paid plan to a higher one:** the plan fee difference for the rest of the month is prorated and invoiced at once.

Usage is never prorated: it is billed in arrears at the end of the month at the rates of your plan.

For example, a workspace that upgrades from Free to Starter on April 16 pays half the monthly Starter fee on April 16 for April 16 to April 30. On May 1 it pays the full Starter fee for May plus any operations above the Starter allowance used from April 16 to April 30.

## Downgrade to a lower paid plan

1. Open **Workspace Settings > Billing**.
2. Select **Manage subscription** and choose the lower plan.

The downgrade is scheduled for the end of the current billing period. Until then, the workspace keeps the higher plan and its limits, and you've already paid for that month. Downgrades are not prorated, and no part of the current month's fee is returned.

Downgrading mid-month does not reduce usage already counted in the current period. That usage is billed at the end of the month.

## Cancel a paid plan

Cancelling moves the workspace back to the Free plan at the end of the current billing period.

1. Open **Workspace Settings > Billing** for the workspace you want to cancel.
2. Select **Manage subscription**.
3. Choose the **Free** plan.
4. Select **Schedule cancellation**.

The paid plan stays active, with its limits, until the end of the current billing period. No part of the period is credited back. A cancellation made in Console doesn't end the plan early, so there is no unused time to credit: you keep the paid plan, and pay for it, until the end of the period you already paid for.

After the period ends, the workspace is on the Free plan and the Free plan limits apply from then on. See [Usage limits and blocks](/console/billing/usage-limits-and-blocks) for what happens when a Free workspace reaches them.

### The final invoice after cancelling

Usage is billed in arrears, so you receive one more invoice after the paid plan ends. It contains the usage above your plan's allowance during the last billing period, and it does not include another plan fee.

### Undo a scheduled cancellation

Until the period ends, you can keep the paid plan. Open **Workspace Settings > Billing** and choose **Continue subscription**. The plan then renews on the 1st as usual.

### Subscribe again later

If you upgrade again after the cancellation has taken effect, select **Upgrade**. Console treats it as a new paid subscription and opens checkout, with the first plan fee prorated for the rest of the month.

### Delete the workspace after cancelling

A workspace on a paid plan can't be deleted. Cancel the plan first, wait for the cancellation to take effect at the end of the period, and make sure no invoice is unpaid. See [Delete your account, workspace, or database](/console/account/delete-account).

## Workspaces billed through Vercel

If the workspace was created through the Vercel Marketplace, you can change its plan in Vercel or in Console under **Workspace Settings > Billing**. A change made in one place is applied in the other. The timing rules are the same: upgrades apply at once, and downgrades and cancellations take effect at the end of the billing period.

A Vercel-billed workspace and a workspace you created in Console are separate workspaces, and cancelling one does not cancel the other. A Vercel-billed workspace can't also have a card-billed Prisma subscription, so being charged by both Prisma and Vercel always means you have two workspaces.

A workspace can't be switched from Vercel Marketplace billing to direct Prisma billing, or the other way around, and support can't move it for you. To change how you're billed, cancel the plan on one workspace and sign up for a plan on the other. Move your data first: export each database with `pg_dump` and import it into a database in the new workspace. See [Export your data](/postgres/database/export-data).

See [Vercel Marketplace billing](/console/billing/vercel-marketplace) for how these workspaces differ.

## Common questions

### I cancelled, so why was I charged again?

Two cases are common. Either you cancelled partway through a period, and the charge is the final invoice for usage during that period, billed in arrears, or the charge comes from a different workspace that is still on a paid plan, so check **Workspace Settings > Billing** in every workspace you belong to.

### If I downgrade mid-month, do I get money back?

No. Downgrades take effect at the end of the billing period and aren't prorated. You keep the higher plan until then.

### Why was my first invoice less than the plan fee?

When you upgrade from Free partway through a month, the first plan fee covers only the days left in that month.

### Do I get credit for the rest of the month when I cancel?

No. The paid plan stays active until the end of the period you've paid for, so there's no unused time to credit.

### Can I cancel and have it take effect immediately?

Cancellations made in Console take effect at the end of the current billing period. To stop new usage charges before then, delete the databases and apps you no longer need. A [spend limit](/console/billing/spend-limits) also blocks your Prisma Postgres databases once usage charges reach it.

### Why can't I change my plan?

A workspace with an unpaid invoice can't change plan. Pay the invoice first, then try again. See [Invoices and payments](/console/billing/invoices-and-payments).

### I cancelled by mistake. Can I keep my plan?

Yes, as long as the current period hasn't ended. Open **Workspace Settings > Billing** and choose **Continue subscription**.
