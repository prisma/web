This page answers the security and compliance questions that come up when you evaluate or run Prisma Postgres and the Prisma Console: certifications, agreements, where your data lives, how it's protected, and who to contact.

## Certifications

Prisma holds a **SOC 2 Type II** report and an **ISO 27001:2022** certification, and supports GDPR and HIPAA. Coverage depends on your plan; for the compliance each plan includes, see the [pricing page](https://www.prisma.io/pricing).

On the Business plan, download the latest SOC 2 Type II report and ISO 27001 certificate from **Workspace Settings > Compliance** in the Console. For control details, policies, and other security documentation, see the [Prisma Trust Center](https://trust.prisma.io). Questions about these documents go to [compliance@prisma.io](mailto:compliance@prisma.io).

## HIPAA and Business Associate Agreements

Prisma signs a HIPAA Business Associate Agreement (BAA) with customers on the **Pro** and **Business** plans. Free and Starter workspaces aren't eligible; upgrade to Pro or Business first. On those plans the signed agreement carries no additional cost and covers all Prisma offerings, including Prisma Postgres and storage buckets.

You can review the standard BAA in **Workspace Settings > Compliance** under **HIPAA**. To request a signed BAA, email [compliance@prisma.io](mailto:compliance@prisma.io) with the subject "Signed BAA Request", from your account email, and include:

- your company's legal name and address,
- your account email,
- the name and email address of the person who will sign.

Prisma sends its standard agreement for electronic signature.

## GDPR and data subject requests

### Data processing agreement

Prisma's Data Processing Agreement (DPA) forms part of the [Terms of Service](https://www.prisma.io/legal/terms), so it applies automatically when you accept the terms; you don't need to sign a separate copy. Where Prisma processes personal data on your behalf, Prisma acts as your processor under the DPA.

To download the DPA, go to **Workspace Settings > Compliance** in the Console and select **View document** under **DPA**. It's available on every plan. Send questions about the DPA to [compliance@prisma.io](mailto:compliance@prisma.io).

### Data subject requests

Data subject requests under GDPR, such as access or erasure requests, are handled by Prisma's data protection officer.

Send data subject requests and GDPR questions to [dpo@prisma.io](mailto:dpo@prisma.io). Prisma responds within 30 days, or sooner where applicable law requires. See the [Privacy Policy](https://www.prisma.io/legal/privacy) for your rights under GDPR, UK GDPR, and US state privacy laws.

To delete your own account and data, you don't need to file a request. Go to **Account Settings > Profile > Delete Account**. This deletes your user account and every workspace where you're the only member. For a deletion request that goes beyond this, email [dpo@prisma.io](mailto:dpo@prisma.io). See [Delete your account](/console/account/delete-account) for what's deleted and what blocks deletion.

Prisma keeps some records after deletion where needed for legal, tax, and accounting obligations, such as invoices. Retention periods vary by data category and are available on request from [dpo@prisma.io](mailto:dpo@prisma.io).

## Data location

Each Prisma Postgres database runs in the region you choose when you create it. You choose the region per database. Choosing a region determines where your resources are provisioned, but per the [Terms of Service](https://www.prisma.io/legal/terms) it doesn't by itself create a data-residency commitment. See [Regions](/postgres/database/regions) for the list of regions and how to pick one.

Regions are the same for every account; Prisma can't enable an extra region for a single workspace.

Prisma Data, Inc. transfers personal data internationally under the EU-U.S., UK, and Swiss Data Privacy Framework. See the [Privacy Policy](https://www.prisma.io/legal/privacy).

## Encryption

### In transit

Connections to Prisma Postgres require TLS: SSL is mandatory, and connection strings use `sslmode=require`. See [Connecting to your database](/postgres/database/connecting-to-your-database).

TLS terminates at Prisma's connection proxy, and the proxy connects to your database inside Prisma's infrastructure. That traffic is encrypted whenever it travels between machines; the only unencrypted step is between processes on the same machine, so your data never crosses a network unencrypted.

Because the database only sees that last local connection, the `pg_stat_ssl` view inside your database reports `ssl = false` for your session even though your connection is encrypted. This is expected, and you don't need to change any settings.

### At rest

Prisma Postgres data is stored on disks encrypted with AES-256, and backups are stored in object storage that is also encrypted at rest with AES-256. No configuration is needed. Prisma manages the encryption keys; customer-managed keys aren't available.

### Isolation

Each Prisma Postgres database runs in its own virtual machine, isolated from other customers' databases. Databases don't share a Postgres server or process with other customers.

## Payment security

Prisma uses Stripe to process card payments. Your card details are stored by Stripe, and Prisma doesn't store card numbers.

If you see an unexpected charge attempt on a card you use for Prisma, check your invoices in **Workspace Settings > Billing**. If the charge doesn't match an invoice there, it didn't come from Prisma; contact your card issuer. See [Invoices and payments](/console/billing/invoices-and-payments).

Workspaces created through the Vercel Marketplace pay Vercel, and Prisma holds no payment method for them.

## Report a security vulnerability

Report security vulnerabilities privately to [security@prisma.io](mailto:security@prisma.io). Don't open a public GitHub issue or post details in Discord.

Include:

- the affected product, URL, or package and version,
- steps to reproduce or a proof of concept,
- the impact you observed and your assessment of severity,
- any disclosure timeline you're working to.

Prisma acknowledges reports within five business days, then coordinates a fix and disclosure timeline with you and, with your permission, credits you in the advisory. Prisma doesn't offer a bug bounty or other compensation for reports. For vulnerabilities in Prisma ORM packages, you can also use [GitHub private vulnerability reporting](https://github.com/prisma/orm/security/advisories/new). See the published [security policy](https://github.com/prisma/orm/security/policy) for the full disclosure process.

## Subprocessors

Prisma publishes its current list of subprocessors in the [Prisma Trust Center](https://trust.prisma.io). For questions about subprocessors or the [DPA](#data-processing-agreement), email [compliance@prisma.io](mailto:compliance@prisma.io).

## Impersonation and scams

Prisma support never asks you to pay a third party, send cryptocurrency, or "activate a wallet". Someone who asks you to, including in Discord or through a "ticket" link, isn't Prisma. Report it to [support@prisma.io](mailto:support@prisma.io).

## Common questions

### Does Prisma have a DPA?

Yes. The DPA is part of the Terms of Service and applies on every plan. Download it from **Workspace Settings > Compliance**. See [Data processing agreement](#data-processing-agreement).

### Is Prisma SOC 2 compliant?

Yes. Prisma holds a SOC 2 Type II report and an ISO 27001:2022 certification. On the Business plan, download them from **Workspace Settings > Compliance**. See [Certifications](#certifications).

### Will Prisma sign a BAA for HIPAA? Does it cost extra?

Yes, on the Pro and Business plans. On those plans the BAA has no additional cost and covers all Prisma offerings, including Prisma Postgres and storage buckets. Email [compliance@prisma.io](mailto:compliance@prisma.io) with your company's legal name and address, your account email, and the signer's name and email.

### How do I make a GDPR erasure request?

You can delete your own account under **Account Settings > Profile > Delete Account**. For a formal data subject request, email [dpo@prisma.io](mailto:dpo@prisma.io). Prisma responds within 30 days.

### Can I keep my data in the EU?

You can choose an EU region, such as Frankfurt (`eu-central-1`) or Paris (`eu-west-3`), when you create the database, and your database runs there. Choosing a region doesn't by itself create a data-residency commitment; see [Data location](#data-location) and [Regions](/postgres/database/regions).

### Why does `pg_stat_ssl` say my connection isn't encrypted?

TLS ends at Prisma's connection proxy, so the database only sees the local connection from a proxy on its own machine. Your connection from your app to Prisma is encrypted, and so is traffic between machines inside Prisma's infrastructure.

### Did Prisma leak my card details?

Prisma doesn't store card numbers; Stripe does. If a charge doesn't match an invoice in **Workspace Settings > Billing**, it didn't come from Prisma.

### Do you have a bug bounty?

No, Prisma doesn't offer a bug bounty. Report vulnerabilities to [security@prisma.io](mailto:security@prisma.io) under the published disclosure policy. See [Report a security vulnerability](#report-a-security-vulnerability).
