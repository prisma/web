You can sign Prisma's Data Processing Addendum (DPA) yourself from the Prisma Console, without contacting Prisma. The Console also links to Prisma's other compliance documents, such as the HIPAA Business Associate Agreement (BAA) and the SOC 2 Type II report.

## What the DPA is

A DPA is a contract that sets out how Prisma processes personal data on your behalf when you use the Prisma Data Platform. Many companies need a signed DPA with each of their vendors to meet the requirements of the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).

For example, if your application stores your customers' names and email addresses in Prisma Postgres, your legal or procurement team may ask for a signed DPA with Prisma before you go to production.

The DPA is available on every plan, including Free.

## Sign the DPA [#sign-the-dpa]

Only workspace Admins can open the Compliance page. If you have a different role, ask an Admin of your workspace to sign the DPA, or to give you the Admin role. See [Workspace roles and project access](/console/workspace-access) for what each role can do.

1. Open the [Prisma Console](https://console.prisma.io) and switch to the workspace you want the DPA for, with the workspace switcher at the top left.
2. Go to **Workspace Settings** > **Compliance**.
3. In the **DPA** section, click **Sign DPA**. The signing page opens in a new browser tab, on Documenso, the e-signature service Prisma uses.
4. Enter your full name, add your signature, and complete the signing steps.

Documenso emails you the signed DPA when you finish. Keep that email: it is your signed copy, and Prisma does not need to sign anything else for the DPA to be complete.

To read the DPA before you sign it, click **View document** next to **Sign DPA**.

## Other compliance documents

The same **Compliance** page links to these documents. Which ones you can open depends on your workspace's plan. On a plan that doesn't include a document, the page shows an upgrade button in its place.

| Document | What it covers | Plans |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Data Processing Addendum (DPA) | How Prisma processes personal data, for GDPR | All plans |
| HIPAA Business Associate Agreement (BAA) | Prisma's responsibilities when you store protected health information | Pro, Business, Enterprise |
| ISO 27001:2022 certificate | Prisma's information security management system | Business, Enterprise |
| SOC 2 Type II report | Prisma's controls for security, availability, processing integrity, confidentiality, and privacy | Business, Enterprise |

The BAA in the Console is a template. If you need a signed BAA, email [compliance@prisma.io](mailto:compliance@prisma.io) with the subject "Signed BAA Request".

For Prisma's security and privacy practices, see the [Prisma Trust Center](https://trust.prisma.io). For questions about any of these documents, email [compliance@prisma.io](mailto:compliance@prisma.io).
