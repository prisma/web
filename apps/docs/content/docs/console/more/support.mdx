The support you get depends on your workspace's plan. Free and Starter workspaces get help from the community on Discord for technical questions, and Pro, Business, and Enterprise workspaces can write to the support team directly. Billing and data protection requests are handled by email on every plan. Before you open a request, check the [status page](#status-page) if something stopped working, and the [account](/console/account/sign-in) and [billing](/console/billing) pages for self-service answers.

## Support by plan

| Plan          | Where to ask                                                              | Response time             |
| ------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------- |
| Free, Starter | [Discord](https://pris.ly/discord?utm_source=docs&utm_medium=inline_text) | Answered by the community |
| Pro           | [support@prisma.io](mailto:support@prisma.io) or the Console              | 2 business days           |
| Business      | [support@prisma.io](mailto:support@prisma.io) or the Console              | 1 business hour           |
| Enterprise    | A dedicated contact person, 24/7                                          | Custom                    |

For the support tier each plan includes, see the [pricing page](https://www.prisma.io/pricing).

Business hours are Monday to Friday, 9am to 5pm CET.

Dedicated support comes with the Enterprise plan. To discuss it, [contact enterprise sales](https://www.prisma.io/enterprise).

### Free and Starter workspaces

If you email support from a Free or Starter workspace with a technical question, you receive an automatic reply pointing you to Discord. Ask in Discord for questions about using Prisma, such as errors, schema design, or connecting to your database.

Billing help is available on every plan. For an incorrect charge, a refund question, or anything else about an invoice, email [support@prisma.io](mailto:support@prisma.io) from the email on your account, even on Free or Starter. Data protection requests, such as a GDPR erasure request, are also handled on every plan; send them to [dpo@prisma.io](mailto:dpo@prisma.io).

### Paid plans

Email [support@prisma.io](mailto:support@prisma.io) from the email address on your Prisma account, or contact support from the Console. Support shares account-specific details only with someone writing from an email on the account, so writing from another address slows things down.

## Status page

Check the status page first when a database or the Console stops responding: [prisma-status.com](https://www.prisma-status.com). It lists open incidents by product and region.

To get notified about incidents, click **Subscribe to updates** on [prisma-status.com](https://www.prisma-status.com). You can subscribe by email, Slack, webhook, or RSS/Atom feed. The Console also shows a banner while an incident is open.

## Report an outage

Errors such as `Failed to connect to upstream database`, `Can't reach database server`, or `P6008` that start at a specific time, on a database that was working, can mean a platform incident.

1. Check the [status page](#status-page) for an open incident in your database's region. If there is one, the team is already working on it; follow the incident for updates.
2. Check whether your workspace is blocked. A workspace that hit its plan limit or spend limit, or has an unpaid invoice, stops accepting queries, and that often looks like an outage. See [Usage limits and blocks](/console/billing/usage-limits-and-blocks).
3. If neither explains it, contact support with the exact error text, the times it started and stopped in UTC, the region, and the database name or ID.

See [Availability and maintenance](/postgres/database/availability-and-maintenance) for how maintenance and incidents are handled.

## What to include in a support request

Support can answer faster when the first message contains everything needed to find your resources and reproduce the problem. Include:

- the email address on your Prisma account (write from it if you can),
- the workspace, project, and database names, or their IDs,
- the database region,
- the exact error message, copied as text rather than a screenshot,
- when it happened, with timestamps in UTC,
- what you were doing: the command, query, or Console page, and the tool and version you used (for example, the Prisma ORM version),
- for billing questions, the invoice number.

Don't send passwords, full connection strings, or API keys.

## Delete your account

You can delete your account yourself in the Console, without contacting support. See [Delete your account](/console/account/delete-account) for the steps and what can block deletion.

## Impersonation and scams

Prisma support never asks you to pay a third party, send cryptocurrency, or "activate a wallet". Anyone who does, including people offering support "tickets" in Discord, isn't Prisma. Report it to [support@prisma.io](mailto:support@prisma.io).

## Common questions

### I'm on the Free plan. Can I email support?

For billing questions and data protection requests, yes: those are handled by email on every plan. For technical questions, you'll receive an automatic reply pointing you to [Discord](https://pris.ly/discord?utm_source=docs&utm_medium=inline_text), where the community and Prisma team answer questions.

### How quickly will support reply?

It depends on your plan: 2 business days on Pro and 1 business hour on Business, Monday to Friday, 9am to 5pm CET. Enterprise plans get a dedicated contact, 24/7.

### Where can I see if Prisma is down?

On the status page at [prisma-status.com](https://www.prisma-status.com). If nothing is listed but your database stopped responding at a specific time, contact support with the error text, UTC timestamps, region, and database name.

### Can I get notified when there's an incident?

Yes. Click **Subscribe to updates** on [prisma-status.com](https://www.prisma-status.com) and choose email, Slack, webhook, or RSS/Atom.

### My database stopped working but the status page is green. What should I check?

Check whether your workspace is blocked by a plan limit, spend limit, or unpaid invoice; see [Usage limits and blocks](/console/billing/usage-limits-and-blocks). If not, contact support with the details listed in [What to include in a support request](#what-to-include-in-a-support-request).

### How do I delete my account? Do I need to email support?

No. Go to **Account Settings > Profile > Delete Account**. See [Delete your account](/console/account/delete-account).

### Someone in Discord offered to fix my account for a fee. Is that Prisma?

No. Prisma support never asks for payment to a third party or cryptocurrency. Report it to [support@prisma.io](mailto:support@prisma.io).
