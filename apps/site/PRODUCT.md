# Prisma marketing site

## Register

brand

## Users

Developers exploring Prisma's TypeScript ORM, managed Postgres, and app hosting.

## Product Purpose

Explain the Prisma stack and help visitors explore products, read documentation,
compare pricing, and create an account.

## Brand Personality

For the requested April Fools edition: playful, simple, childish. The reference
is a children's picture book and a box of colorful building blocks.

## Anti-references

The April Fools edition should avoid the usual sophisticated glass effects,
muted corporate colors, complex diagrams, and dense marketing copy.

## Design Principles

- Make the April Fools joke obvious on arrival.
- Preserve the original copy, information, navigation, and calls to action.
- Use building-block visuals to carry the April Fools joke.
- Use Comic Sans for all text, including controls and code examples.
- Keep real product destinations and calls to action usable.
- Carry the same playful identity across the marketing site.
- Add quick interaction feedback and gentle illustration motion without
  hiding content; pause decoration offscreen and support reduced motion.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Keep text readable against the bright palette, preserve keyboard navigation,
label icon controls, and support small screens and reduced motion.
